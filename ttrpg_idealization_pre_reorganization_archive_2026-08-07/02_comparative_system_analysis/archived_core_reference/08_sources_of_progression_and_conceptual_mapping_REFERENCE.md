# Master Sources of Progression & Conceptual Mapping

To establish absolute clarity when building our universal check pools across the 20 levels (`and 6 Tiers of Power`), we must inventory every possible source of character and monster progression.

When an actor (`PC, NPC, or Monster`) declares an action or defends against a threat, exactly **Eight Building Blocks (`Sources of Progression`)** interact on the character sheet and the battlefield.

---

## 1. The 8 Sources of Progression (`Master Inventory`)

```
[ Universal Actor Progression Ecosystem ]
  ├── Innate Identity & Biological Foundations
  │     ├── 1. Core Attributes (`STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA`)
  │     └── 2. Race / Ancestry / Species (`Human, Elf, Ogre, Dragon`)
  │
  ├── Training, Advancement & Specialization
  │     ├── 3. Class Archetype & Levels (`1 to 20 across 6 Tiers`)
  │     ├── 4. Skill / Competency Ranks (`Untrained to Legendary in Action Domains`)
  │     └── 5. Feats (`Modular Vector Traits: General, Combat, Racial, Class`)
  │
  └── External & Dynamic Battlefield Context
        ├── 6. Equipment & Magic Items (`Weapons, Armor Soak, Durability Slots`)
        ├── 7. Tactical & Environmental Context (`Flanking, Cover, Range Tiers`)
        └── 8. In-World Resource Expenditure (`Stamina/Focus Burn, Heroic Item Sacrifice`)
```

## 3. How the 8 Sources Assemble a Check Pool (`Step-by-Step Synthesis`)

To see exactly how these 8 sources synthesize without a single line of $+X/-X$ arithmetic, let's trace a **Level 8 Veteran Human Fighter (`Trained in Swords`)** attacking an **Ogre Brute (`Soak d10 Hide+Apron`)** while flanking:

```
[ Step 1: Base Die Size (`X`) from Attribute + Competency Rank ]
  ├── Source 1 (`Core Attribute`): Strength is `STR d10` (upgraded at Level 4 and 8).
  └── Source 4 (`Competency Rank`): Domain is `Expert in Combat Mastery` (+1 Die Step-Up to `d12`, Competency Floor = 5).
  ──► CURRENT DIE SIZE: `d12` (Floor 5)

[ Step 2: Pool Volume (`N`) from Base + Class + Competency + Tactics ]
  ├── Base Check Pool: `2d`
  ├── Source 3 (`Class Baseline`): Martial Fighter Class grants `+1B` (+1 die volume).
  ├── Source 4 (`Competency Rank`): Expert Rank grants `+1B` (+1 die volume).
  └── Source 7 (`Tactical Positioning`): Flanking the Ogre grants `+1B` (+1 die volume).
  ──► TOTAL DICE: `2d + 3 Boons` = `5d12 keep highest` (`Max Pool Cap reached!`)

[ Step 3: Exertion & Feat Override (`Action Economy & Step-Up`) ]
  ├── Source 8 (`In-World Exertion`): Fighter spends `1 Stamina` to perform a Die Step-Up (`5d12` -> `5d12 over-cap pool`).
  └── Source 5 (`Class Feat`): Fighter declares `Power Strike [1A]` (-1 die volume (`4d12`), but Weapon Damage Die steps `d8 -> d10`).
  ──► FINAL ROLLED POOL: `4d12 keep highest` (Guaranteed Floor 5) against Ogre Parry (`3d10`).
  ──► IF HIT: Rolls `Damage Die d10 vs Ogre Soak d10` (`and spends any earned [SET] tokens to Impale or Trip the Ogre!`)
```

### 3.1 Why This 8-Block Ecosystem is the Ultimate Mathless Standard
By giving every single progression source (`Attributes, Race, Class, Competency, Feats, Equipment, Tactics, and Exertion`) a specific, distinct job on our check pool knobs (`Die Size, Pool Volume, Floor, or Action/Resource cost`), players experience deep, 3.5e-level tactical character customization where every choice directly shapes the physical dice in their hand!

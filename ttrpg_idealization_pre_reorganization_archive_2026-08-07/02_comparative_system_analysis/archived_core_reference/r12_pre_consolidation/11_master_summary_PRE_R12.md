# Master Executive Summary: Top-to-Bottom System Architecture

This document provides a concise, comprehensive, top-to-bottom briefing of our **Idealized Mathless High Fantasy TTRPG System** (`as of Decision DEC-035`). 

It consolidates all mechanics, progression tracks, check engines, combat layers, and resource pools into a single unified view. This overview serves as our definitive design baseline to review, trim, or refactor specific subsystems (`such as streamlining quick disruptions or adjusting specific class talents`).

---

## 1. Core Philosophy & Universal Actor Schema

- **High Fantasy Baseline (`DEC-014`):** Level 1 characters start as **Trained Heroes (`above average civilian baselines`)**. They are competent professionals capable of reliable execution and heroic survival.
- **Strictly Zero Table-Side Arithmetic (`DEC-001`):** Action resolution compares rolled die face values (`d4` to `d12`) directly against Target Numbers (`DCs 2 to 12`) or opposed die faces (`max vs max`). There is zero addition or subtraction of linear numerical modifiers (`no +BAB, no +Stat mod, no -4 Power Attack`).
- **Strictly Zero Meta-Currencies (`DEC-006`):** All heroic luck, effort, and survival run strictly on in-world character tracks (`Stamina, Focus, Vancian Spell Slots, and Equipment Durability Sacrifices`).
- **Universal Actor Schema (`DEC-011`):** Every single entity in the game world (`PCs, human NPCs, Goblins, Ogres, Dragons`) is built from the exact same four foundational blocks:
  $$\text{Actor Block} = \text{Race / Ancestry} + \text{Class Levels (`1-20 across 6 Tiers`)} + \text{Skills (`Specific Allocations`)} + \text{Feats (`Vector Traits`)}$$

## 2. The Check Engine (`Tri-Vector Pools & Competency Floors`)

When any actor declares a check or attack, their dice pool (`NdX keep highest`) is generated by three variables:

```
[ Assembling a Check Pool: The 3 Mechanical Variables ]
  ├── 1. Die Size (`X` — d4 to d12) ──────────────► Governs Capability Ceiling & Impact (`Core Attribute + Competency Step Shift`)
  ├── 2. Pool Volume (`N` — 1d to 5d) ─────────────► Governs Reliability & Multi-Beat Count (`Base 2d + Class Boon + Tactical Boons`)
  └── 3. Competency Floor (`F` — 0, 3, 5, 7, 9) ──► Governs Guaranteed Minimum Face (`Investment-Driven Whiff Immunity`)
```

### 2.1 Competency Ranks (`Specific Skills under 5 Umbrellas — DEC-020 & DEC-030`)
Competency Ranks measure specific player investment in **Individual Specific Skills** (`Stealth, Lockpicking, Arcana, Nature, Diplomacy, Blades, Bows`) grouped under our 5 Action Mastery Domain Umbrellas (`Combat, Survival, Subterfuge, Lore, Social`), independent of overall character level:
- **0. Untrained (`Zero Investment`):** Base Pool `2dX keep highest`, **Floor = 0** (`Can whiff on routine tasks`).
- **1. Trained (`Basic Professional Focus`):** Pool Volume `3dX` (`+1B`), **Floor = 3** (`100% immune to failing DC 3`).
- **2. Expert (`Dedicated Specialization`):** Pool Volume `3dX` (`+1B`) + **1 Die Step-Up (`X+1`)**, **Floor = 5** (`100% immune to failing DC 5`).
- **3. Master (`Elite Grandmastery`):** Pool Volume `4dX` (`+2B`) + **2 Die Step-Ups (`X+2`)**, **Floor = 7** (`100% immune to failing DC 7`).
- **4. Legendary (`Mythic Apex`):** Pool Volume `5dX` (`+3B — Max Pool Cap`) + **3 Die Step-Ups (`X+3`)**, **Floor = 9** (`100% immune to failing DC 9`).

### 2.2 Boons, Banes & Die Stepping (`DEC-016 & DEC-017`)
- **Boons (`+1B, +2B`) & Banes (`+1X, +2X`):** Adjust **Pool Volume only (`N`)** by adding or removing dice of the SAME SIZE (`2d8 + 1B -> 3d8 keep highest`). Banes remove dice (`2d8 + 1X -> 1d8 single roll; +2X -> 2d8 keep lowest / Disadvantage`). They cancel 1-to-1 before rolling. Max pool ceiling is `5dX`.
- **Die Step-Up (`X+1`) & Step-Down (`X-1`):** Adjust **Die Size only (`X`)**. Spending **1 Stamina (`or Focus`)** performs an on-demand **Die Step-Up (`e.g., 2d6 -> 2d8`)** to overcome hard DC ceilings or push physical limits. Over-encumbrance or exhaustion imposes a **Die Step-Down (`d10 -> d8`)**.

## 3. The Three Check Modes (`Tri-Modal Resolution — DEC-015`)

The Game Master applies the appropriate resolution engine based on intuitive battlefield reality:

| Check Mode | Core Comparison Formula | When to Apply (`Intuitive Trigger`) | Action Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mode 1: Fixed DC** | $\max(\text{Pool}) \geq \text{Static DC (`2 to 12`)}$ | **Static obstacles, environmental hazards, downtime crafting clocks, and uncontested feats.** | Climbing a sheer cliff (`DC 7`), picking an unattended master lock (`DC 8`), forging a steel blade (`DC 5`). |
| **Mode 2: Pure Opposed** | $\max(\text{Attacker}) > \max(\text{Defender})$ | **Dynamic, chaotic clashing between two active opponents where difficulty is purely opponent effort.** | Melee combat strikes where defender actively parries/evades (`Combat vs Parry`), wrestling/grappling, active stealth vs searching. |
| **Mode 3: Hybrid (`DC Floor`)**| $\max(\text{Attacker}) > \max(\text{Defender})$<br>**AND** $\max(\text{Attacker}) \geq \text{DC Floor}$ | **Complex, technical actions carrying an intrinsic physical/spatial difficulty floor against active resistance.** | Ranged attacks at extreme distance / heavy cover (`Must beat Evasion AND clear Distance DC Floor`), channeling high-tier spells against target Resilience (`Must beat Resilience AND clear Spell Complexity Floor`). |

## 4. Attributes & Paired Defenses (`DEC-021 & DEC-022`)

Characters possess the **Classic Six Ability Scores (`STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA`)** as step dice (`d4 to d12`). Two paired attributes combine directly to form the baseline check pool (`1dAttr1 + 1dAttr2 keep highest`) for our three saving throw defense layers:

```
       [ Classic 6 Ability Scores ] ──────────► [ Derived Paired Defense Pool (`1dAttr1 + 1dAttr2 keep highest`) ]
  ├── Strength (STR) + Constitution (CON)    ──► Fortitude Pool (`Purely Physical Resilience vs Poisons/Disease`)
  ├── Dexterity (DEX) + Intelligence (INT)   ──► Reflexes Pool (`Hybrid Physical + Mental Evasion vs Fireballs/AoE`)
  └── Wisdom (WIS) + Charisma (CHA)          ──► Willpower Pool (`Purely Mental/Spiritual Resilience vs Mind Blasts/Curses`)
```

- **Good vs. Poor Class Saves (`DEC-029 Track 3`):** If a class has a Good Save in 3.5e (`e.g., Fighter Fortitude, Wizard Willpower`), the class awards **`+1B (`+1 die volume -> 3dX`)`** at Level 1, and elevates to **`+2B (`4dX, Floor 5/7`)`** at Level 9 (`Master Tier Entry`). Poor saves roll standard `2dX` paired pools (`Floor 0/3`).

## 5. Action Economy & Turn Structure (`DEC-008 & DEC-031`)

- **3-Action Turn Budget (`[A][A][A]`):** On their turn, characters spend 3 action points flexibly across Single Actions (`[1A] Stride, Strike, Raise Guard`), Two-Action Activities (`[2A] Cast Spell, Sudden Charge`), or Three-Action Surges (`[3A] Whirlwind Strike`).
- **Multi-Attack Fatigue:** Making multiple physical attacks on the same turn imposes **Stamina/Pool Fatigue (`+1 Bane Die on 2nd attack, +2 Banes on 3rd`)**, eliminating 3.5e's $-5/-10$ subtraction and stationary full-attack paralysis.
- **Active Defense Budget (`[1R] + Stamina Burn`):** Every character gets **1 Free Defensive Reaction (`[1R]`)** per round (`Active Parry or Shield Intercept`). Additional active parries/evades against swarming enemies **cost 1 Stamina per attempt (`Parry Fatigue`)**. When Stamina hits $0$, active defense collapses (`Parry Pool = 0`), forcing reliance on passive armor Soak!
- **Dual-Mode Spatial Engine (`Specific vs. 5-Tier — DEC-031`):** Natively supports 5-foot squares (`VTT grid`) and **5-Tier Abstracted Zones (`Tier 0 Close, Tier 1 Near, Tier 2 Medium, Tier 3 Far, Tier 4 Distant`)**. 
  $$\text{1 Standard Stride ([1A] Action) = Move 30 Feet = Shift Exactly 1 Distance Tier}$$

Six concrete in-world tracks govern character lifespan, exertion, and camp tension across a **Dual-Frequency Pacing Model**:

| Resource Track | Derivation & Maximum Capacity | Depletion Triggers | Recovery Rule (`10-Minute Breather vs. 8-Hour Camp`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **2. | **3. ## 7. Combat Resolution, Criticals & Wounds (`DEC-005, DEC-034`)

When an attack connects, it interacts with our 4 defensive layers (`Reflexes DEX+INT, Parry Combat Pool, Resilience Fort/Will, and Soak Rank Armor Die`). **Soak (`d4 Cloth to d12 Plate`) is checked post-hit (`Damage Die Face vs Soak Die Face`):**

```
[ Attacker Hits Defender ] ──► Compare: `Damage Die Face vs Soak Die Face`
  ├── Damage < Soak ──────────► Absorbed (`0 Wounds`). Defender loses 1 Stamina from jarring shock.
  ├── Damage == Soak ─────────► Superficial Shock. Defender loses 2 Stamina (`0 Wounds`).
  ├── Damage > Soak ──────────► Standard Hit. Defender loses normal Vitality (`HP`) equal to Damage face shown. Can inflict quick minor disruptions (`Bleed, Off-balance`). *No Anatomical Wounds!*
  └── Critical Hit (`d100 Check`) ► Severe Lethal Hit (`DEC-034`). Double Vitality loss (`Damage x 2`) + Severe Anatomical Wound (`1d8 Matrix`).
```

### 7.1 The $d100$ Critical Threat Profile (`DEC-034`)
Every weapon has an explicit **Critical Threat Profile (`Crit Profile %`)**:
- **Standard (`Maces, Longswords`):** `10% Crit Profile` (`01-10 on d100`).
- **Keen / Finesse (`Rapiers, Daggers`):** `15% to 20% Crit Profile` (`01-15 or 01-20 on d100`).
- **Heavy Picks / Siege:** `5% Crit Profile` (`01-05 on d100`), but `x3 or x4 Vitality multiplier`.

When attacking, roll your step pool (`say, 3d10`) and **1 percentile die ($d100$) simultaneously**. If the check succeeds AND $d100$ falls within the weapon's Crit Profile, it is a **Critical Hit**!

### 7.2 Hit Location & Anatomical Wounds (`DEC-034 & DEC-044`)
When a **Natural Critical Hit connects (`via d100 threat profile`)**, double Vitality loss is dealt (`Max Damage Face x 2`), AND you roll **`1d8` randomly on our Anatomical Matrix** (`1 Head, 2-3 Chest, 4 Abdomen, 5 Shield Arm, 6 Weapon Arm, 7-8 Legs`) to apply structural limb severance or concussion!
- **The Called Shot Maneuver (`Forcing a Wound without a Crit`):** A character can execute a **Called Shot maneuver** (`spending [1A] + taking +1 Bane Die on attack, OR spending 3 Multi-Successes post-hit`). If successful, **it does NOT deal double damage (`inflicts standard shown/rolled Damage Face vs Absorption`)**, BUT the attacker **chooses the exact anatomical body part targeted (`Head, Chest, Arm, Leg`) and triggers that location's severe Anatomical Wound Condition (`Concussion, Dropped Weapon, Prone`)!**

### 7.3 Multi-Success Maneuvers & Pool Trade-offs (`DEC-042 & DEC-043`)
To execute universal combat maneuvers without token currencies (`[SET] removed — DEC-042`), maneuvers trigger via **Multi-Success Counts (`Required Successes S`)** and **Check Pool Trade-offs (`+1 Bane Die`)**:
- **1 Success (`Highest face beats defender`):** **Standard Hit (`Damage vs Absorption`)**. Inflicts quick minor disruptions (`Bleed cut, Shaken`). *No Prone, Disarm, or Wounds.*
- **2 Successes (`Two dice equal/exceed defender's highest face`):** **Superior Hit (`Standard Hit + 1 Universal Maneuver`)**. Automatically execute 1 universal maneuver (`Trip, Disarm, Shove, Overextend`) off the dice!
- **3+ Successes (`Three dice equal/exceed defender's highest face`):** **Overwhelming Hit (`Standard Hit + Major Maneuver OR 2 Maneuvers`)**. Execute 2 maneuvers OR force an **Anatomical Called Shot (`chosen body part condition`)**!
- **Tactical Trade-off Knobs (`DEC-043`):** If a player *knows* they need a maneuver (`say, Disarm or Called Shot`), they can declare it before rolling by accepting **$+1\text{ Bane Die (`-1 die volume: 4d10` $\to$ `3d10`)}$** to guarantee the maneuver on any 1-Success hit, OR spend a dedicated action point (`[1A] Maneuver check / [2A] Precision Strike`).

## 8. Class Advancement Tracks & 20-Level Progression (`DEC-013 & DEC-035`)

Character and monster growth from Level 1 to 20 (`and Epic 21+`) is organized across **6 High Fantasy Tiers (`Trained Lvl 1-4 -> Veteran Lvl 5-8 -> Master Lvl 9-12 -> Hero Lvl 13-16 -> Legend Lvl 17-20 -> Mythic Lvl 21+`)**. 

All 5 classic D&D 3.5e class tracks translate cleanly across these tiers (`see 02_comparative_system_analysis/archived_core_reference/09_dnd_3_5e_class_advancement_tracks_mapping_REFERENCE.md for full Good/Okay/Bad matrix`):
1. **Hit Points (`HP`):** Bounded increments (`Good +2 HP/lvl, Okay +1 HP/lvl, Bad +1 at odds`).
2. **Base Attack Bonus (`BAB`):** Check pool step/volume growth (`Good reaches Expert Floor 5 at Lvl 5, Master Floor 7 at Lvl 9, Legend Floor 9 at Lvl 15`).
3. **Saving Throws (`Fort, Ref, Will`):** Good saves award `+1B` at Level 1 and `+2B` at Level 9. Poor saves roll base `2dX` paired pools.
4. **Caster Level (`CL`):** Vancian slots (`Full 1st-9th Good vs Hybrid Okay`). Tradition Competency sets check pool (`Trained 3dX/Floor 3 to Legend 5dX+3 Steps/Floor 9`). Focus scales `+3/lvl Good vs +1/lvl Bad`.
5. **Skill Allocations:** High-skill (`Good`) gets 4 allocations at Lvl 1 (`+1/lvl`); Medium (`Okay`) gets 3 allocations at Lvl 1; Heavy/Combat (`Bad`) gets 2 allocations at Lvl 1 (`+1 at evens/odds`).

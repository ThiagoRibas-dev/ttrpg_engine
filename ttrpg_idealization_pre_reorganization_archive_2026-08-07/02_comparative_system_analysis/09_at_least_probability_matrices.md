# At-Least Probability Matrices

**Status:** Phase 1 probability research artifact.
**Purpose:** Exact probabilities that a pool of `N` same-sized dice produces at least one face meeting or exceeding threshold `T`.

## Definition

Each cell answers:

> What is the probability that the highest face in an `NdS` pool is at least `T`?

Formula:

```text
P(max ≥ T) = 1 − ((T − 1) / S)^N
```

These matrices do not apply Competency Boons and Banes, DC labels, Automatic Successes, opposed-roll rules, or Required Successes. They are the baseline raw-dice reference.

Rows cover `1dS` through `(S−1)dS`, because this artifact examines pool volumes up to one less than the die size. Columns cover every face threshold from 1 through S.

## Reading the Matrices

- `≥ 1` is always 100% because every die has a face of at least 1.
- `≥ S` is the probability of rolling the maximum face at least once.
- Increasing pool volume raises reliability without changing the numerical ceiling.
- Increasing die size raises the possible threshold ceiling.

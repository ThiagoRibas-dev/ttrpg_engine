# Pulverized Master Skill Matrix & Modular Class/Path Architecture

This document archives our **historical Pulverized Master Skill Matrix (`65 Atomic Skills`)** across all physical, mental, and hybrid attribute linkages, along with our **4-to-5 Level Modular Class/Path Hybrid Architecture (`DEC-047`)** and **Player-Created Backgrounds (`DEC-045`)**.

Crucially, this 65-skill atomic matrix is our master inventory. **Whether our game uses all 65 skills directly on the character sheet (`Option A: GURPS/CoC style`), or consolidates them into ~28 grouped skills (`Option B: D&D/PF2e style`), has been superseded as the official sheet architecture by the revised Domain-and-Activity model (`DEC-049`). The 65 entries remain a historical design inventory and conversion reference.**

---

## 1. Player-Created Backgrounds & Modular Class/Path Hybrid (`DEC-045 & DEC-047`)

### 1.1 Player-Created Backgrounds (`DEC-045`)
Rather than choosing from a restrictive list of 10 hardcoded backgrounds (`e.g., Acolyte, Soldier`), **the player creates their own unique Background (`Origin`) at Level 1** by selecting two foundational elements:
1. **One Primary Attribute Focus (`STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, or CHA`):** This attribute automatically starts at **`d8`**, and steps up automatically across campaign milestones (`stepping up at Levels 5, 9, 15, and 20 -> d8 -> d10 -> d12 -> d12+1 -> Apex d12+2`), guaranteeing core role capability (`DEC-039`).
2. **One Primary Skill Proficiency (`Trained Rank, Floor 3`):** Chosen from any skill *not* restricted by Class (`e.g., Blacksmithing, Diplomacy, Survival, or Sleight of Hand`).

### 1.2 The 4-to-5 Level Modular Class/Path Hybrid (`DEC-047`)
Instead of a single rigid 1-to-20 linear class (`e.g., Fighter 1-20`), every Class—including **Base Classes (`Warrior, Rogue, Caster, Priest`)**, **Specialized Classes (`Berserker, Paladin, Wizard, Ranger`)**, and **Master Prestige Classes (`Blade Dancer, Chronomancer, Archmage`)**—consists of exactly **4 to 5 Levels (`1 Tier Band`)**:

```
[ 20-Level Character Build: Layering 4-Level Modular Class Bands ]
  ├── Tier 1 (Levels 1 to 4): Base Class / Novice Path (`e.g., Warrior Lvl 1-4 OR Rogue Lvl 1-4`)
  ├── Tier 2 (Levels 5 to 8): Expert Class / Prestige Path (`e.g., Berserker Lvl 1-4 OR multiclass Wizard Lvl 1-4`)
  ├── Tier 3 (Levels 9 to 12): Master Class (`e.g., Blade Dancer Lvl 1-4 OR continue Berserker`)
  ├── Tier 4 (Levels 13 to 16): Heroic Class (`e.g., Juggernaut Lvl 1-4 OR Realm Commander Lvl 1-4`)
  └── Tier 5 (Levels 17 to 20): Legendary Class (`e.g., Mythic Avatar Lvl 1-4 OR Multiversal Titan Lvl 1-4`)
```

When a character completes a 4-level band (`Tier Boundary`), they can either advance into a higher-tier prestige class OR multiclass laterally into another base class (`say, a Warrior taking 4 levels of Rogue or Wizard`).

## 3. Historical Options Superseded by the Revised Architecture (`DEC-049`)

With our **65 itemized atomic skills** completely documented and verified above, we as designers and players face our exact choice for how to structure the character sheet:

- **Option A: The Pure Pulverized Sheet (`All 65 Skills Available — GURPS / Call of Cthulhu style`)**
  - Every single one of the 65 skills is listed directly on the character sheet or chosen individually.
  - *Pros:* Ultimate simulationist precision (`A master of *Heavy Swords [1]* might be totally untrained in *Polearms [3]* or *Finesse Blades [2]*`).
  - *Cons:* Requires giving characters more skill allocations (`say, 25 to 40 allocations across 20 levels`), and requires tracking many distinct lines.
- **Option B: The Consolidated Hybrid Sheet (`~28 Actionable Skills — D&D 3.5e / PF2e style`)**
  - We group atomic skills that share identical attribute linkages and physical execution (`say, combining #15 Climbing, #16 Swimming, and #17 Jumping into **Athletics [STR+DEX]**; combining #30 Lockpicking and #31 Mechanical Traps into **Mechanics [DEX+INT]**; keeping #24 Riding and #26 Beast Taming distinct`).
  - *Pros:* Clean 2-page character sheet (`~28 skills total`), while retaining 100% of our physical+mental hybrid attribute linkages!

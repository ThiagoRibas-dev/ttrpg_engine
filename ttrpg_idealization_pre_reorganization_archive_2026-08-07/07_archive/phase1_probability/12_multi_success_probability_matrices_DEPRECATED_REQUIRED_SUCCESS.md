# Multi-Success Probability Matrices

**Status:** Phase 1 research artifact; no DC target or Required Success target is canonical.

## Definition

Each cell answers:

> What is the probability that at least `R` dice in an `NdS` pool meet or exceed threshold `T`?

Formula:

```text
P(at least R successes) = Σ[k=R..N] C(N,k) p^k (1−p)^(N−k)
p = (S−T+1)/S
```

This is the mathematical model for `DC T (R)`. It is distinct from “keep highest R”; Required Successes count every die face meeting the Target Number.

The matrices cover standard dice d4, d6, d8, d10, and d12, with pools from 1dS through SdS and Required Successes from 1 through the pool size.

**Status:** Canonical Divine-Domain access reference.  
**Scope:** The 28 canonical Spell Tradition Skill names and scopes, plus the framework mapping of legacy-style Divine Domains to Tradition access. ## 1. Divine-Domain Access Procedure

```text
Cleric base:
  Grants access to the Divine Tradition Skill.

Each Divine Domain:
  Grants access to one additional specified Tradition Skill.
  May also grant Skills, Permissions, Feats, Devotions,
  spell-acquisition rules, or other explicit Features.
```

A Domain grants access and immediately raises its associated Tradition Skill to **Trained**. If that associated Tradition is already Trained or higher when the Domain is granted, instead raise that associated Tradition by one Competency Rank, up to the character’s current Character-Level Rank cap. This is a **Domain Tradition Grant**, not a purchased Skill Investment. If the associated Tradition is already at the current cap, the Domain grants no additional Rank; its other stated Features and permissions still apply.

Protected progression and Skill Investment procedures belong to the progression and spellcasting owners. If more than one Domain grants the same Tradition, resolve each Domain Tradition Grant when that Domain is gained. Each Domain still grants its own stated non-Tradition Feature or acquisition permission.

A Domain is a class-content access package. School, Tradition(s), and Trait(s) remain the universal spell-record axes; see the Magic and Spellcasting owner.

### Key boundaries

```text
Good is not Light.
Evil is not Void.
Law is not merely Protection.
Chaos is not merely Destruction.
Air is not Sound.
Dragon is Arcane; Scalykind remains Nature.
```

## 4. Domain Feature Examples

The following examples clarify the access-package boundary; they do not create complete individual Domain entries.

```text
War Domain:
  grants War Tradition access and its Domain Tradition Grant;
  may grant the Combat Mastery Skill for the deity’s favored weapon;
  may grant a martial Permission, Feat, Devotion, or spell-acquisition rule.

Sun Domain:
  grants Light Tradition access and its Domain Tradition Grant;
  may grant an anti-undead Rite or a radiance-specific Feature.

Healing Domain:
  grants Life Tradition access and its Domain Tradition Grant;
  may grant an explicit healing, treatment, or restoration Feature.

Rune Domain:
  grants Artifice Tradition access and its Domain Tradition Grant;
  may grant a literacy, inscription, ward, or rune-item Permission.
```

## 5. Scope Boundary

This file establishes the Skill catalogue and Domain-to-Tradition access framework. It does not establish:

- A complete playable Cleric Domain catalogue.
- Deity portfolios.
- Domain-granted Competency Ranks.
- Individual Domain Features, Devotions, Feats, or spell-acquisition quantities.
- Individual spell lists, spell entries, or prepared-spell limits.
- Whether a particular Class table grants an additional Magic Mastery-only Skill Investment at a given level.

Those are Class, Domain, spell, and content entries governed by their respective canonical owners.

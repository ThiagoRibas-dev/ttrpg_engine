# Maneuver Activity Matrix — Draft

**Status:** Brainstorming draft; non-canonical.  
**Purpose:** Provide one editable matrix for selecting the baseline maneuver set, assigning broad timing and Action expectations, and later writing mechanics. Nothing in this document establishes a rule unless separately approved and recorded in a canonical document and the decision log.

## Reading the Matrix

- **Family** identifies the maneuver’s broad timing role; it is not itself a rule.
- **Suggested Action** is a starting proposal for discussion, not a locked Action cost.
- **Mechanic direction** describes the intended job of the maneuver, not its final resolution procedure.
- **Dependencies** identify rules that should be stable before the maneuver becomes final.
- **Soften Blow** and **Ward Self** are included for complete navigation, but their current procedures are already canonical in `../04_simulationist_subsystems/04_combat_maneuvers.md`.

## Baseline Matrix

| Family | Maneuver Activity | Typical Skill / Access | Suggested Action | Description | Draft mechanic direction | Dependencies / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reaction | **Parry** (including Shield Block) | Suitable Combat Mastery Skill; shield or parrying implement where applicable | Reaction | Intercept or deflect a physical attack with a weapon, shield, body, or suitable defense. | Oppose or otherwise answer an eligible physical Attack. Shield Block is a Parry mode, not a separate baseline maneuver. | Defense layers; shield and Damage Absorption framework; Stamina cost, if any, declared by the specific Parry mode. |
| Reaction | **Evasion** | Reflexes or suitable movement permission | Reaction | Avoid an external threat through movement, footwork, positioning, or reflex. | Answer an eligible threat that can be avoided rather than intercepted. | Reflexes; movement; Stamina cost, if any, declared by the specific Evasion rule. |
| Reaction | **Interpose** | Suitable Combat Mastery Skill, shield, body, or explicit Permission | Reaction | Place oneself or a suitable defense between an eligible attack and another creature, object, route, or position. | Redirect, intercept, or contest an attack that the interposer could plausibly Parry or Block. | Targeting rules; reach; cover; shield framework. Not limited to allies. |
| Damage response | **Soften Blow** | Stamina | No Action; once per Attack | Exert to reduce external physical damage. | **Canonical:** spend 1 Stamina per prevented Damage Box after applicable damage is determined and before Vitality is marked; a damaging Attack still marks 1 Damage Box. | Current canonical procedure; eligible sources target Reflexes, Parry, or Damage Absorption. |
| Damage response | **Ward Self** | Essence | No Action; once per Attack | Use internal, magical, or spiritual force to reduce damage from an inner threat. | **Canonical:** spend 1 Essence per prevented Damage Box after damage is determined and before Vitality is marked; a damaging Attack still marks 1 Damage Box. | Current canonical procedure; eligible sources target Fortitude or Willpower; damage only. |
| Turn Activity | **Full Defense** | Any creature able to defend | 3 Actions | Forego ordinary offense to focus entirely on survival and positioning. | Improve access to defensive options, protection, or defensive readiness for the round. | Parry, Evasion, Interpose, and action/reaction limits must be finalized first. |
| Turn Activity | **Exert** *(working name for physical Die Step-Up)* | Stamina; relevant physical Activity | No separate Action proposed; modifies the Activity being performed | Push a physical effort beyond ordinary capability. | Spend Stamina to Step-Up the dice for the stated physical Activity. | Decide whether it is a maneuver, an Activity modifier, or a universal Stamina Permission; exact cost and scope open. |
| Turn Activity | **Charge** *(includes Overrun)* | Suitable movement and Combat Mastery Skill | 2 Actions | Move aggressively into engagement, then Strike, Shove, Trip, or force passage through a foe. | Combine Movement with a forceful offensive or positional result; Overrun is a Charge outcome rather than a separate entry. | Movement, terrain, reach, and trade-off against defense or positioning. |
| Turn Activity | **Grapple** | Athletics, Martial Arts, or suitable Combat Mastery Skill | 1 Action | Establish a close hold that restricts movement and enables later control. | Apply Grabbed, Restrained, or a project-equivalent state; support later Escape. | Restraint-condition hierarchy; unarmed/free-hand requirements; size rules. |
| Turn Activity | **Trip** | Athletics, Martial Arts, or suitable Combat Mastery Skill | 1 Action | Knock a target down. | Apply Prone or another ground-position condition. | Prone and standing procedures; size/reach considerations. |
| Turn Activity | **Shove** | Athletics, Martial Arts, shield, or suitable Combat Mastery Skill | 1 Action | Force a target away, into terrain, or out of position. | Move the target by a defined distance tier or create a positional consequence. | Spatial engine; collision, ledges, and size rules. |
| Turn Activity | **Disarm** | Suitable Combat Mastery Skill | 1 Action | Force a held weapon, shield, tool, focus, or object out of effective use. | Make the item unavailable, dropped, displaced, or otherwise subject to retrieval. | Ready/Stow/Interact procedures; item and shield rules. |
| Turn Activity | **Rally** | Leadership or Diplomacy | 1 Action | Coordinate, encourage, direct, or steady allies. | Grant a temporary tactical Permission, remove/limit a suitable morale state, or organize allied positioning. | Morale conditions; ally targeting; avoid generic numeric bonuses. |
| Turn Activity | **Intimidate** | Intimidation | 1 Action | Frighten, pressure, threaten, or force hesitation. | Apply a fear or pressure condition, or impose a defined tactical restriction. | Fear-condition ladder; immunity and repeated-use limits. |
| Turn Activity | **Taunt** | Deception or Performance | 1 Action | Bait, mislead, provoke, distract, or create a tactical opening. | Create a defined opening against the target without predicting or compelling its future choice. | Off-Guard/flanking/opening procedure; Mental, Auditory, or Visual Tags may later apply. |
| Turn Activity | **Assess** | Insight, Knowledge, or Lore | 1 Action | Observe, study, identify, or recall useful information about a creature, object, hazard, location, or ongoing effect. | Reveal information without compelling, predicting, or locking a target’s future action. | Insight: immediate behavior or focus; Knowledge: systematic facts and vulnerabilities; Lore: specific history, identity, culture, or reputation. |
| Strike variant | **Power Attack** | Suitable Strike and weapon/unarmed attack | Modifies a Strike; proposed +1 Action | Commit to a more forceful blow. | Exchange an explicit cost or drawback for increased Damage Boxes, armor pressure, forced movement, or another forceful outcome. | Weapon damage and Damage Absorption framework; exact trade-off open. |
| Strike variant | **Cleave** | Suitable melee Strike; weapon or permission as applicable | Follows a qualifying Strike; no separate Action proposed | Carry a decisive melee attack into another adjacent target. | Grant a follow-up Strike or transfer force after a qualifying hit, defeat, or other trigger. | Multi-attack fatigue; reach; target adjacency; qualifying trigger. |
| Strike variant | **Called Shot** | Suitable Strike; explicit requirement or trade-off | Modifies a Strike; exact Action cost open | Deliberately target an anatomical location. | On success, choose the Wound location and apply its listed Wound Condition rather than relying on a random Wound Roll. | **Partly canonical:** distinct from Natural Critical; exact trigger/trade-off remains open. |
| State response | **Escape** | Athletics, Acrobatics, Martial Arts, or other applicable Skill | 1 Action | Break free from a Grapple, Restrained state, Immobilized state, or similar control. | End or reduce the controlling state, potentially with movement. | Grapple/restraint hierarchy; terrain and magical-restraint exceptions. |
| State response | **Execution** | Suitable weapon, unarmed method, or explicit Permission | 3 Actions proposed | Kill an Incapacitated target. | A successful Execution kills the target. | **Partly canonical:** target must be Incapacitated; exact requirements, action cost, and resolution remain open. |

## Candidate Additions — Do Not Add Yet

These concepts are promising but should remain deferred until their dependency exists or the baseline list proves insufficient.

| Candidate | Why it is interesting | Why it is deferred |
|---|---|---|
| **Bleed** | Creates persistent pressure after cutting or piercing trauma. | Better owned by Wound, weapon Trait, poison, or Critical procedures. |
| **Sunder / Damage Equipment** | Creates weapon and armor pressure. | Requires Equipment Durability and item-damage rules. |
| **Pin Weapon** | Makes hooks, chains, forks, nets, and specialized weapons tactically distinct. | Requires weapon Traits and restraint/item procedures. |
| **Entangle** | Supports nets, vines, chains, webs, and magical bindings. | Better as a weapon, terrain, spell, or effect outcome. |
| **Reposition / Pull / Drag** | Adds granular forced movement beyond Shove and Grapple. | Revisit only if Charge, Shove, and Grapple leave a tactical gap. |
| **Compel Surrender** | Supports nonlethal victories and tactical surrender. | Requires morale, NPC-intent, and social-conflict procedures. |
| **Aim / Take Cover / Ready** | Important tactical choices. | General Action Economy or spatial procedures, not maneuver Activities. |
| **Mounted maneuvers** | Supports charges, lance use, and mounted control. | Requires mounted/vehicle framework. |

## Questions for the Next Pass

1. Is **Exert** the preferred name for physical Die Step-Up, or should it remain `Die Step-Up` until an alternative is selected?
2. Are all three social Turn Activities—Rally, Intimidate, and Taunt—part of the baseline set?
3. Does **Assess** belong in the combat maneuver catalogue or the general Activity framework with a combat application?
4. Is the proposed Action distribution suitable before exact mechanics are written?
5. Which baseline entries require an explicit Skill, weapon Trait, shield, free hand, movement, or target-state Requirement?
6. Should **Full Defense** cost all three Actions, or should it have a smaller Action cost plus additional limits?

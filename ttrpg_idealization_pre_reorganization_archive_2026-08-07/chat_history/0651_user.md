# Turn 651 — User

Proposed repair procedure:

- Each item has Durability points or marks rather than a large HP number.
- An item has a functional/nonfunctional threshold and becomes Broken at 0.
- A Simple Repair Activity restores 1 Durability per minute with the right tools and can be performed during a Short Breather.
- A Broken item requires materials and a Crafting check. Its recipe defines the repair materials and amount, less than full creation materials.

# Turn 622 — User

For control conditions and maneuvers:

- Prone: Standing is a Movement Activity that should trigger Attack of Opportunity; Prone movement is Crawl up to 5 feet / 1 square; Prone Actors do not count for flanking. Requested guidance on Deflect, Evasion, Reflex, and melee-versus-ranged interaction.
- Do not use Grabbed; Grapple applies Restrained directly.
- Restrained should be a limited-Action state modeled after D&D 3.5e and PF2e.
- Immobilized prevents Movement Activities and otherwise allows normal action unless another rule says otherwise.
- Grapple: Martial Arts versus target Reflexes; target must be within one size category; impose a Circumstance Bane against larger targets and a Circumstance Boon against smaller targets; success applies Restrained; Escape ends the Grapple.
- Escape: 1 Action and 1 Stamina; Acrobatics or Athletics versus the grappler’s Martial Arts; other sources of Restrained or Immobilized define their own escape procedure.

- Tradition access and prerequisites.

Just like in D&amp;D 3.5e, where a class level comes with a list of skills you can invest on for that class level, our skills (including the tradition skills) will work the same.

So when taking a level of Cleric, you'll be able to invest skill points in Traditions A, B, and C, when taking a level of Wizard, you'll be able to invest in Traditions B, E, and F. So a character that takes one level of Cleric, then a level of Wizard might have 1 point in Tradition A, one point in Tradition F and 2 points in Tradition E.

This approach is pretty granular and kind of coplex (have to keep track of each tradition's caster level individually), but we can begin with highly granular and complex then brainstorm the best way to simplify it down.

# Turn 628 — User

Control-maneuver decisions:

- Attack of Opportunity: Trigger—an adjacent creature uses a Manipulate or Movement Activity; Cost—1 Reaction; Effect—make 1 Strike against the triggering Actor.
- Trip: Cost—1 Action and 1 Stamina; Effect—roll a Strike against the target’s Athletics or Acrobatics; on success apply Prone.
- Shove: use the same Size Boon/Bane rules as Grapple. Shoving a target against a wall causes Unarmed Strike damage to that target. Shoving a target against another Actor causes Unarmed Strike damage to both targets.
- Disarm: defer for a dedicated pass.
- Multiple grapplers: penalties do not stack. When trying to move, a Grappled Actor rolls against both Grappling creatures.
- Grapple movement contest: Athletics or Martial Arts.
- Other Restrained / Immobilized sources define their own effects; a Net may apply both Immobilized and Restrained.

# Turn 93 — AI-Designer

Divine versus Celestial

I would not replace Divine with Celestial as a direct one-to-one substitution.

Divine

“Divine” describes the source relationship:

Gods
Sacred powers
Cosmic principles
Oaths
Worship
Religious authority
Higher intelligences

It can include:

Celestial beings
Fiends
Death gods
Nature gods
Abstract divine principles
Deities of darkness, chaos, or destruction
Celestial

“Celestial” describes a more specific cosmic or heavenly source:

Angels
Heavenly beings
Stars
The upper planes
Radiance
Cosmic order
Benevolent or transcendent forces

It tends to imply:

Goodness or purity
The heavens
Angelic intermediaries
Light
Law
Protection

That makes Celestial thematically excellent, but narrower and more morally loaded.

Recommendation

Use:

Divine as the broad tradition.
Celestial as a specialist tradition, subtradition, mantle, or source.

For example:

text
Divine Tradition:
  Celestial
  Infernal
  Chthonic
  Ecclesiastical
  Primordial
  Cosmic

Alternatively, if we decide that the game’s “Divine” magic is specifically channeled through celestial or higher beings, then Celestial can replace Divine. But we should recognize that this removes the clean category for:

Death gods
Fiendish patrons
Dark divinities
Gods of chaos
Gods of nature
Non-celestial cosmic powers

My preferred wording is:

Divine / Theurgic: Magic channeled through a higher power, sacred covenant, deity, cosmic principle, or supernatural patron.

Then Celestial becomes one possible Divine specialization.

Natural versus Primal

This is a closer choice.

Natural

“Natural” suggests:

The ordinary physical world
Ecology
Biology
Plants
Animals
Weather
Non-artificial processes

It is clear and accessible, but may sound too scientific or mundane for high fantasy.

Primal

“Primal” suggests:

Ancient forces
The raw elements
Pre-civilized power
Natural law
Instinct
The world before civilization
Primordial creation

It feels more mythic, but can be less immediately precise.

Recommendation

Use:

Primal as the formal tradition name.
Natural as a mantle or specialization within it.

Possible Primal branches:

Natural World
Elements
Beasts
Seasons
Weather
Growth
Decay
Primordial Forces

If the desired tone is less mythic and more grounded, Natural is perfectly viable. But for a system spanning Country, World, Cosmic, and Divine tiers, Primal will age better thematically.

A possible compromise is:

Primal / Natural

This could be the formal name during development, with one term eventually chosen for the final publication.

The Ardent Mantles

The Ardent mantles are extremely useful for our system, but I would not make them additional spell traditions.

They are better understood as a third axis:

text
Tradition = how magic is accessed.
School = what technical function the spell performs.
Mantle = what fundamental concept or domain the spell expresses.

For example:

text
Spell: Fireball

Traditions: Arcane, Elemental, War
School: Evocation
Mantles: Elements, Energy, Destruction
Delivery: Area, Projectile

Or:

text
Spell: Raise Dead

Traditions: Divine, Spirit, Death, Blood
School: Necromancy
Mantles: Life, Death, Repose, Creation
Delivery: Ritual

The Mantle layer is extremely useful for:

Spell access.
Specialist feats.
Class and path identity.
Spell lists.
Lore interactions.
Opposition between magical forces.
Mythic advancement.
Ardent-style character construction.

7. My Strongest Recommendation

I would formally adopt the following vocabulary:

text
Schools:
  The eight D&D-compatible technical classifications.

Traditions:
  Modular magical access and specialization packages.

Mantles:
  Fundamental conceptual domains, inspired partly by the Ardent system.

Traits:
  Specific descriptors such as Fire, Curse, Healing, Ritual, Area, or Summoning.

Magic Mastery Skills:
  The actual proficiency skills used to cast through traditions.

And for the names:

text
Arcane = manipulation of raw magic through study, formula, and will.
Divine = channeled power through deity, covenant, cosmic authority, or higher power.
Primal = direct power of nature, life, elements, and primordial forces.

Use Celestial as a Divine subtradition or specialist tradition, and use Natural World as a Primal mantle rather than replacing Primal entirely.

The Ardent mantles should become the foundation of the system’s Mantle layer, not a replacement for schools or traditions.

# Turn 128 — User

Again, you are misreading the table.

A character with "Spellcasting Progression Level" 1 (such as a lvl 1 wizard) has 4 1st circle spell slots.

A character with "Spellcasting Progression Level" 4 (such as a lvl 4 wizard) has 4 1st circle spell slots and 2 2nd circle spell slots. That's the bottom and top of the rained Tiers.

Talking about a full caster where their progression aligns 1 to 1 with their character level:

Trained is progressions from lvl 1 to 4, which means circles 1 and 2 spell slots.

Veteran is progression from lvl 5 to 8, which means gaining access to circles 3 and 4 spell slots.

etc etc. Much like a D&D 3.5e Wizard or Cleric.

>6. Proposed depletion states		

Resource	At 0	What remains possible

Vitality	Incapacitated and subject to stabilization or execution / Wound consequences	No ordinary Actions or Reactions; damage beyond this point should threaten severe Wounds and death (roll a d8)

Stamina	Winded	Basic Actions and Free Guard remain available; cannot spend Stamina for Soften Blow, extra active defenses, Die Step-Ups, Surges, or other Stamina costs (these are design explanation, not rules text, since the results arise naturally)

Essence	Drained	Cannot spend Essence for Ward Self, metamagic, magical exertion, or Essence-powered features; Spell Slot casting remains possible unless the specific spell requires Essence (these are design explanation, not rules text, since the results arise naturally)

The idea of rolling a d8 is that each face will correspond to a body part, nothing, or death like so:

1. Death

2. Head.

3. Chest.

4. Abdomen.

5. Shield or off-arm.

6. Weapon arm.

7. Legs.

8. Nothing

So a creature dies when :

1. Vitality hits 0

2. Creatures is Executed (maneuver a la Coup de Grace from D&amp;D 3.5e) OR Creature is hit and d8 lands on 1, or twice in a roll on a number from 2 to 7.

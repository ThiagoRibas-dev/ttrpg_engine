# Turn 96 — User

I agree with the vast majority of what you proposed with the following caveats :

Alchemy: Let's not make it a magical tradition in and on itself. Instead it's something that should be governed by craft and knowledge skills to create chemical and pseudo-magical effects.

Psychic: I'm torn on having this as a magical tradition, or copying D&D 3.5e and having Psychic/Psionic power as a distinct, non-vancian system.

Blood: I'd call it Life to contrast with Death.

These points aside I think this list is amazing. The overlap between these traditions and the traditional spell classification system will make for very interesting spell selection.

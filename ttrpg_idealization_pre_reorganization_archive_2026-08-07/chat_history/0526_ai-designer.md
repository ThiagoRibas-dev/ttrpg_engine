# Turn 526 — AI-Designer

Recommended using the existing typed Boon/Bane framework for maneuver-derived Conditions rather than making all maneuvers untyped or reserving types for equipment and magic. Proposed Rallied as a Morale Boon, Shaken as a Circumstance Boon to attackers, and Distracted as a Circumstance Bane. This permits intentional cross-type combinations, such as Rallied plus Shaken, while preventing uncontrolled stacking from repeated uses of the same condition. Recommended no new Martial type.

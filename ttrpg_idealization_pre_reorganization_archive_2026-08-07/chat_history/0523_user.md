# Turn 523 — User

Full Defense: the Character-Level cap applies separately to each defensive roll.

Exert applies to every Activity that spends Stamina, for now.

Charge permits one Strike and one of either Grapple or Shove by default. Valid combinations include Strike then Grapple, Strike then Shove, and Shove then Strike when reach permits it. Grapple then Shove and Shove then Grapple are not default Charge combinations.

Execution: the attacker has to damage the defender for Execution to work. A downed Actor at 0 Vitality has no defenses.

Cleave: remove the adjacent-line requirement. Cleave can attack up to 3 creatures in the attacker’s Reach.

Taunt gives the target a Bane on its next Strike or Defense Maneuver check until the end of its next turn.

Rally gives the target a Boon on its next Strike, Defense Maneuver, or Resistance check (Fortitude or Willpower) until the end of its next turn.

Intimidate gives a Boon to any Attackers targeting the intimidated Actor until the end of its next turn.

These social effects should be described in terms of one or more Conditions that other sources, such as fear spells, can also inflict.

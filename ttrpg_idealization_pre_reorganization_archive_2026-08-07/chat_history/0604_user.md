# Turn 604 — User

Record this as a note in PROJECT_STATUS.md:

Looking at our progression, we get a lot of dice in our pools. The original idea was that higher proficiency grants more dice, improving the chance of high results and resistance to Banes.

A possible future re-evaluation is to make Attributes determine Die Size and Competency/Proficiency determine the roll floor. Under that alternative, stronger and more proficient Actors would not necessarily roll more dice; they would roll better results with a guaranteed floor.

# Turn 159 — AI-Designer

Main Mechanical Pillars
1. Mathless Step-Pool Resolution

The core engine avoids table-side arithmetic.

Characters roll pools such as:

text
2dX keep highest

where:

Attribute determines die size.
Competency Rank determines pool volume and Competency Floor.
Class, feats, tactics, equipment, and conditions provide discrete permissions or Boons/Banes.
Results compare directly against fixed DCs or opposed rolls.

The system avoids linear modifiers such as:

text
+2 attack
-4 Power Attack
+3 circumstance bonus
+BAB
+Ability Modifier
2. Die Steps, Boons, Banes, and Floors

The main resolution tools are:

Die Step-Up: d8 → d10.
Die Step-Down: d10 → d8.
Boons: Add same-size dice, such as 2d8 + 1B → 3d8 keep highest.
Banes: Remove dice or force worse results.
Competency Floors: Training can prevent results below a fixed threshold.

Competency is independent from Character Level.

text
Untrained: Floor 0
Trained: Floor 3
Expert: Floor 5
Master: Floor 7
Legendary: Floor 9

The system retains the classic six attributes:

Strength
Dexterity

They also derive resources and defenses, such as:

Vitality.

4. Universal Actor Schema

Every actor is constructed from the same five building blocks:

text
Race/Ancestry
Background/Origin
Class and Prestige Classes
Skills
Feats

This applies to:

PCs.
Human NPCs.
Goblins.
Ogres.
Dragons.
Bosses.

Other monsters.

The difference between a PC and a monster is primarily in their selected building blocks and presentation, not in completely unrelated rules.

5. High Fantasy Tier Progression

The game uses 20 character levels across five mortal tiers, plus Mythic advancement:

Tier	Levels	Scope
Trained	1–4	Local
Veteran	5–8	Regional
Master	9–12	Country
Hero	13–16	World
Legend	17–20	Cosmic
Mythic	21+	Divine

Tier boundaries provide major advancement opportunities, while levels inside each tier provide incremental progress.

6. Three-Action Tactical Economy

Each character receives:

text

Actions can be used for:

Attacking.
Striding.
Casting.
Drawing or using equipment.
Maneuvers.
Defensive actions.
Tactical positioning.
Multi-action abilities.

Movement uses five spatial tiers:

text
Close
Near
Medium
Far
Distant

One Stride shifts exactly one distance tier.

7. Active Defenses and Layered Protection

The system replaces one universal Armor Class with multiple defenses.

Core defensive layers include:

Reflexes.
Parry.
Fortitude.
Willpower.
Damage Absorption.

The paired defenses are:

text
Fortitude: Strength + Constitution
Reflexes: Dexterity + Intelligence
Willpower: Wisdom + Charisma

Damage Absorption is resolved after a successful attack rather than functioning as a universal hit-avoidance number.

8. Differential Combat and Wounds

Combat is not merely damage against a large pool of hit points.

The system uses:

Opposed attack and defense rolls.
Multi-success outcomes.
Maneuvers.
Natural critical hits.
Called shots.
Anatomical wound conditions.
Damage Absorption.
Vitality loss.
Conditions such as Prone, Concussion, Bleed, and impaired limbs.
Natural Critical

A natural weapon critical:

Deals increased Vitality loss.
Rolls randomly on the anatomical wound matrix.
Produces a severe location-based condition.
Called Shot

A Called Shot:

Does not deal double damage.
Lets the attacker choose the body part.
Applies the corresponding anatomical condition.

These are deliberately different mechanics.

9. Universal Combat Maneuvers

Maneuvers are available to everyone rather than being locked behind large feat chains.

Examples include:

Trip.
Disarm.
Shove.
Grapple.
Overextend.
Bleed.
Called Shot.

They are triggered through:

Multi-success results.
Dedicated maneuver actions.
Deliberate trade-offs such as accepting a Bane.

This preserves Mythras-style tactical agency while retaining the mathless pool engine.

10. In-World Resources Instead of Meta-Currencies

The system has no out-of-world reroll or narrative currencies.

Important in-world resources include:

Vitality.
Stamina.
Essence.
Spell Slots.
Equipment Durability.
Provisions and other campaign resources.
Stamina

Used for:

Physical exertion.
Active defensive effort.
Martial surges.
Die Step-Ups.
Strenuous movement.
Essence

The renamed magical/supernatural resource.

Used for:

0th-Circle spells.
Metamagic.
Casting stability.
Emergency casting.
Turn or Rebuke Undead.
Smites.
Devotion Feats.
Other supernatural features.

Essence remains separate from Stamina.

11. Specific Skills Organized into Domains

Skills represent transferable areas of competence, not individual procedures.

Current Domains include:

Combat Mastery.

Subterfuge and Perception.
Knowledge and Technical Practice.

Activities such as:

Camp Fortification.

use one or more relevant skills, tools, Lore, Knowledge, class abilities, and feats rather than automatically becoming standalone skills.

12. Domain Architecture

Domains are more than organizational headings.

They may eventually interact directly with:

Class features.
Prestige Classes.
Feats.
Equipment.
Prerequisites.
Domain actions.
Monster design.
Training and advancement.

However, Domains currently do not provide a second proficiency rank. Individual Skills remain the source of check competence.

13. Modular Class and Prestige-Class Advancement

Classes and Prestige Classes are level-banded but use level-by-level tables.

Every class table may define:

Vitality progression.
Combat progression.
Defense progression.
Skill allocations.
Spell Slot Advancements.
Tradition access.
Spell acquisition.
Essence features.
Feats and class abilities.

Prestige Classes use the same mechanical framework as Base Classes. Their distinguishing feature is access requirements.

14. Spellcasting as Shared Vancian Magic

Ordinary spellcasters use:

One universal preparation system.
One shared daily Spell Slot pool.
Spell Circles.
Fixed spell lists by Tradition.
Class-based spell acquisition methods.

Classes do not own separate spell lists.

A Cleric’s Death spells and a Wizard’s Death spells come from the same Death Tradition list.

15. Spell Slot Progression

Classes grant Spell Slot Advancements in their level-by-level tables.

The total accumulated advancements determine the character’s:

Spell Slot Progression Level

That value selects the row on the Reference Good Slot Progression.

Examples:

text
Full caster:
  Advances every class level.

Mediocre caster:
  Advances every other class level.

Bad caster:
  Advances every four class levels.

Noncaster:
  Does not advance Spell Slot Progression.

Multiclassing adds all completed Spell Slot Advancements from all classes and Prestige Classes.

17. Alchemy and Psychic Power

Alchemy is not a magical Tradition.

It is governed by:

Knowledge — Alchemy and Pharmacology.
Craft — Alchemy.
Tools.
Reagents.
Downtime.
Equipment.

Psychic/Psionic power is provisionally a separate Essence-based supernatural system rather than ordinary Vancian Magic Mastery.

18. Enemy and Encounter Scaling

The system supports three broad enemy-management categories:

Rabble: Fast, disposable opponents.
Underlings: Simplified enemies with limited durability.
Iconic Champions: Full actors using the complete rules.

This keeps encounters tactically meaningful without requiring every enemy to track a full PC-style resource and wound structure.

Overall Mechanical Identity

In one sentence:

This is a high-fantasy, class-and-skill-driven tactical RPG using mathless step pools, opposed defenses, modular advancement, shared Vancian spellcasting, specific domains of competence, layered wounds, and in-world resource pressure.

When Intuition Dictates Mode 3: Applied when attempting complex, highly technical actions that carry an intrinsic physical/spatial difficulty floor, while simultaneously facing an active opponent or passive defense.
Typical Applications:
Extreme Range Archery (Tier 3/4 Distance): Hitting a target at 250 feet (Tier 4 Extreme Range) naturally carries a spatial/ballistic difficulty (e.g., Distance Floor DC 7). Even if the target slips in mud and rolls a 2 on Evasion, an archer who rolls a 5 falls short due to wind and trajectory! To hit, the archer must roll higher than the target's Evasion AND clear the DC 7 Distance Floor.
Casting High-Tier Spells against Resilience: Channeling a complex spell (e.g., Disintegrate or Mind Control) carries an intrinsic arcane resonance check (e.g., Spell Complexity Floor DC 6). If the caster rolls a 4, the spell fizzles out due to magical instability before it even reaches the target, regardless of what the target rolled on Resilience.
Picking a Lock under Active Guard Shoving: Trying to pick a door lock (DC 7 Floor) while a guard actively wrestles or holds the door against you (Opposed Athletics).

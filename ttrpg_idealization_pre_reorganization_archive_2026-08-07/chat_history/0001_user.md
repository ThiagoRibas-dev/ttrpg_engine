bespoke mathless system report.md

Let's idealize a new TTRPG system.

Something that could comport diverse abilities and a wealth of content a la D&D 3.5e or Pathfinder 2e, while being math-less.

We just compare rolls to a DC to see if it's lower or higher, so we'll use something like a dice pool and/or step dice with extra dice (a la advantage, boons and banes, etc) instead of modifiers.

It'll also be somewhat simulationist. So having different types of defenses, skills to model certain "generic" capabilities, no "meta currencies", etc.

And we'd create conversion procedures for content from other games, most of all D&D 3.5e, which will be our main inspiration and anchor, followed by the Demon Lord System.

We'll start with a basic baseline system, then work on the philosophy, inspirations, etc, to then work on the mechanics that reflect these on top of the basic framework.

I attached a document analyzing a couple systems. We'll use it as a starting point and add more vectors of analysis, more systems (including board games like Lands of Evershade, Gloohaven, etc.)

I also attached a document of a self-report from somebody who designed a mathless system we can use as a reference.

For now, begin by organizing everything into a directory and file structure that'll help guide our brainstorm.

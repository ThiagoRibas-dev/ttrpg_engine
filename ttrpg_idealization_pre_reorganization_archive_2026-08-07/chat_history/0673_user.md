# User — 0673

The user clarifies the intended spell-access architecture: Classes do not have a single class-owned spell list or map to only one Tradition. Clerics access the Divine Tradition Skill, and each Divine Domain gives access to an additional Spell Tradition Skill. Divine Domains may also grant other access, such as War granting the Combat Mastery Skill for the deity's favored weapon. The user asks to note that D&D 3.5e Divine Domains may require expansion or reshaping of the Tradition list, and proposes for later consideration that spellcasting Classes and Prestige Classes may grant extra Skill Investments restricted to Domain VII: Magic Mastery.

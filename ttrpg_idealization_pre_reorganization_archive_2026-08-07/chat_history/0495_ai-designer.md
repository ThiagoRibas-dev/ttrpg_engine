# Turn 495 — AI-Designer

Proposed, but did not record as canonical, a six-location Wound-condition draft: Head—Dazed; Chest—Fatigued; Abdomen—Nauseated; either arm—Impaired; Legs—Flat-Footed. The draft uses a d10 1–2 impairment test only for arm-dependent Activities, blocks Essence under Dazed and Stamina under Fatigued, gives Nauseated Activities a +1 Action cost, and makes Flat-Footed movement cost +1 Action while disabling Reflexes and causing flanking exposure. It treats Impaired as a single non-stacking condition regardless of arm count.

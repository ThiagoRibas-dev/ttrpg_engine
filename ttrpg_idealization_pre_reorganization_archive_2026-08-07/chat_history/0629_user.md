# Turn 629 — User

Clarified control-maneuver decisions:

- Attack of Opportunity: Trigger—an adjacent creature uses a Manipulate or Movement Activity; Cost—1 Reaction; Effect—make 1 Strike without the Action cost against the triggering Actor.
- Trip: Cost—1 Action and 1 Stamina; roll a Strike versus the target’s Athletics or Acrobatics; on success apply Prone.
- Shove: Athletics or Martial Arts versus Athletics or Acrobatics; use Grapple’s Size Boon/Bane rules; collision with a wall deals Unarmed Strike damage to the target; collision with another Actor deals Unarmed Strike damage to both Actors.
- Disarm remains deferred.
- Multiple grapplers: penalties do not stack; a Grappled Actor trying to move rolls against both Grappling creatures.
- Grapple movement contest uses Athletics or Martial Arts.
- Other Restrained / Immobilized sources define their own effects; a Net may apply both Immobilized and Restrained.

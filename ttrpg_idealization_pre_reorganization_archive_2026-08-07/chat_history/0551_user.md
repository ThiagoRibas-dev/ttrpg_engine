# Turn 551 — User

Stabilization uses Knowledge—Anatomy and Healing against DC 5. A success stabilizes the Actor at 0 Vitality and unconscious; it does not heal a Wound.

A Knowledge—Anatomy and Healing check improves the next Long Rest: success adds one Wound healed to that Long Rest, and each voluntarily accepted +5 DC adds one additional Wound healed.

Spell Slots remain a shared daily resource. Recovery and preparation happen during and after a Long Rest. There is no default Spell Slot preparation or recovery using Essence, and no default exceptional Slot restoration.

Alright, I like the Recommended Axes for Our System.

We'll keep the 8 schools (for backwards compatibility), which are technical classifications of the spells, then we'll have the traditions, which are used for spell access and specialization a la SotDL, rather than PF2e where one class = one tradition, with spells belonging to two or more traditions each.

We'll also keep the distinction between Arcane (manipulate raw magic through study, intuition, etc) and Divine (channeled from a higher power, nature, etc), although these names can change (cast vs channeled?) to avoid overlap.

With that in mind, what's a decent list of traditions we could come up with?

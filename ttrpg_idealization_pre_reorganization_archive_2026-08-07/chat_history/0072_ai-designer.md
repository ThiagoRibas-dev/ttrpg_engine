Pulverized Skill Matrix & Modular Class/Path Architecture

To ensure our skill system achieves maximum simulationist realism before consolidating into an actionable tabletop mechanics list, we must pulverize general capabilities into their Atomic (Pulverized) Elements, mapping them across single attributes and Physical + Mental Hybrid Linkages (STR/DEX/CON + INT/WIS/CHA).

Furthermore, we formalize our Player-Created Backgrounds (DEC-045) and our 4-to-5 Level Modular Class/Path Hybrid Architecture (DEC-047), uniting D&D 3.5e multiclassing with Shadow of the Demon Lord (SotDL) path freedom across our 20 levels.

1. Player-Created Backgrounds & Modular Class/Path Hybrid (DEC-045 & DEC-047)
1.1 Player-Created Backgrounds (DEC-045)

Rather than choosing from a restrictive list of 10 hardcoded backgrounds (e.g., Acolyte, Soldier), the player creates their own unique Background (Origin) at Level 1 by selecting two foundational elements:

One Primary Attribute Focus (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, or CHA): This attribute automatically starts at d8, and steps up automatically across campaign milestones (stepping up at Levels 5, 9, 15, and 20 -> d8 -> d10 -> d12 -> d12+1 -> Apex d12+2), guaranteeing core role capability (DEC-039).
One Primary Skill Proficiency (Trained Rank, Floor 3): Chosen from any skill not restricted by Class (e.g., Blacksmithing, Diplomacy, Survival, or Sleight of Hand).
1.2 The 4-to-5 Level Modular Class/Path Hybrid (DEC-047)

Instead of a single rigid 1-to-20 linear class (e.g., Fighter 1-20), every Class—including Base Classes (Warrior, Rogue, Caster, Priest), Specialized Classes (Berserker, Paladin, Wizard, Ranger), and Master Prestige Classes (Blade Dancer, Chronomancer, Archmage)—consists of exactly 4 to 5 Levels (1 Tier Band):

text
[ 20-Level Character Build: Layering 4-Level Modular Class Bands ]
  ├── Tier 1 (Levels 1 to 4): Base Class / Novice Path (`e.g., Warrior Lvl 1-4 OR Rogue Lvl 1-4`)
  ├── Tier 2 (Levels 5 to 8): Expert Class / Prestige Path (`e.g., Berserker Lvl 1-4 OR multiclass Wizard Lvl 1-4`)
  ├── Tier 3 (Levels 9 to 12): Master Class (`e.g., Blade Dancer Lvl 1-4 OR continue Berserker`)
  ├── Tier 4 (Levels 13 to 16): Heroic Class (`e.g., Juggernaut Lvl 1-4 OR Realm Commander Lvl 1-4`)
  └── Tier 5 (Levels 17 to 20): Legendary Class (`e.g., Mythic Avatar Lvl 1-4 OR Multiversal Titan Lvl 1-4`)
Why this hybrid elevates gameplay: When a character completes a 4-level band (Tier Boundary), they can either advance into a higher-tier prestige class (just like 3.5e Prestige Classes or SotDL Expert/Master paths) OR multiclass laterally into another base class (say, a Warrior taking 4 levels of Rogue or Wizard). Every 4-level class block awards complete, self-contained Good/Okay/Bad advancement tracks (DEC-035) and unique class maneuvers (DEC-043)!
2. The Pulverized Master Skill Matrix (60+ Atomic Skills)

By examining GURPS (100+ skills), Burning Wheel (200+ skills), RuneQuest/Mythras (80+ skills), Call of Cthulhu (60+ skills), and D&D 3.5e (35+ skills), we pulverized general capabilities into their absolute atomic units.

Below is our complete inventory, grouped strictly by Attribute Linkage, with special emphasis on Physical + Mental Hybrid Linkages (STR/DEX/CON + INT/WIS/CHA):

2.1 Pure Physical Linkages (STR, DEX, CON)
Strength (STR):
Lifting / Heaving: Applying vertical or levered force to heavy obstacles (portcullises, boulders).
Smashed Breaching: Ramming doors, breaking chains, or shattering chests with brute force.
Heavy Weaponry: Striking with unbalanced crushing weapons (Greatclubs, Mauls, Heavy Flails).
Dexterity (DEX):
Finesse Blades: Striking with surgical precision weapons (Rapiers, Daggers, Stilettos).
Archery / Marksmanship: Aiming and releasing tensioned ranged weapons (Bows, Crossbows).
Manual Misdirection: Sleight of hand, palm concealing, coin manipulating, and pickpocketing.
Tactile Knot Tying: Tying or escaping intricate rope/chain bindings.
Constitution (CON):
Forced Marching: Maintaining physical pace across days without sleep or proper rations.
Starvation / Dehydration Endurance: Surviving extreme metabolic deprivation.
Pain Suppression: Shrugging off physical shock or continuing action under severe torture.

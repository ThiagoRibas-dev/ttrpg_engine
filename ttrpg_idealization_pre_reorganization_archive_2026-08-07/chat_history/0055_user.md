I guess we can have something like a Background during character creation that's a way to choose that character's physical focus, The one attribute they are exceptional at, starting at a d8, and that will automatically increase.

The every couple levels we let the player choose some other die to increase, with a system similar to PF2E Remaster attribute increases, where increasing from a d4 to d6 or d6 to d8 costs 1 point, but going to a d10 costs two.

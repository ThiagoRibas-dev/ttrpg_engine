## Assumptions Used

```text
Universal Skill Investments:
  Level 1: 6.
  Levels 2–20: 1 each.

Caster-Class benchmark modeled here:
  Each Class Level granting Spell Slot Advancement grants
  1 additional Investment restricted to Magic Mastery.

Domain Tradition Grant:
  A Divine Domain grants its associated Tradition at Trained.
  If that Tradition is already Trained or higher, it instead gains
  one Rank, up to the current Character-Level cap.

Primary Attribute:
  WIS d8 at Levels 1–8; d10 at 9–16; d12 at 17–20.
```

`V / S / E` means Vitality, Stamina, and Essence. Resource grants and totals use the provisional Class-track assignments from the earlier Triel conversion exercise. Exact Features and defense-table entries remain unmodeled.

## Remaining Open Item

The Level 4 universal Investment cannot be Spot: Triel is advancing Cleric at that Level and Spot is not on the modeled Cleric Class Skills. It also cannot advance one of his accessible Tradition Skills in this specific simulation: by Level 4, all six Traditions then accessible to him (Divine, Spirit, Life, Death, Good, and War) are already Trained, and the Level 1–4 cap prevents Veteran advancement.

It must therefore be a different Cleric-eligible non-Magic Skill or an explicit Background / other-access exception. The unsupported former `Craft — Alchemy` choice remains deliberately unfilled.

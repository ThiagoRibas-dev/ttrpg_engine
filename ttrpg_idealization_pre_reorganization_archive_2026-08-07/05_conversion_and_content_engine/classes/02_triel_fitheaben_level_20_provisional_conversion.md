# Triel Fitheaben — Level 20 Provisional Conversion Exercise

**Status:** Non-canonical conversion / stress-test artifact.  
**Purpose:** Test the current Level 1–20 framework against a complex D&D 3.5e divine martial multiclass character. Nothing in this document creates Classes, Feats, spells, magic items, companion rules, or conversion procedures as canon.

## 1. Source Character Snapshot

**Triel Fitheaben** is a Neutral Good human divine warrior and battlefield leader from the Bright Principalities. His projected D&D 3.5e Level 20 progression is:

```text
Cleric 4
Ordained Champion 3
Knight of the Raven 6
Crusader 1
Righteous Cohort of Kiri-Jolith 6
```

The source character’s identity is more important than its 3.5e arithmetic:

- He is a heavily armored sword-and-shield divine champion.
- He is exceptionally resilient in body and will, but not a reflex specialist.
- He is a senior divine caster with a martial / war-prayer emphasis.
- He specializes in the undead, light, healing, protective magic, and weapon-channeling spells.
- He commands, rallies, and protects allies.
- He has a celestial raven harrier and a small set of Crusader maneuvers.

The source progression has **17 ordinary divine spellcasting advancements**:

```text
Cleric 4                 4
Ordained Champion 2–3    2
Knight of the Raven 2–6  5
Righteous Cohort 1–6     6
                           —
Total                   17
```

That directly matches the source sheet’s listed caster level 17 before its exceptional caster-level enhancement.

## 2. Conversion Assumptions

This exercise deliberately makes the minimum necessary provisional assumptions.

| Area | Assumption for this exercise | Canonical status |
|---|---|---|
| Class content | The five source Classes are treated as future framework-test Class / Prestige Class content. | Not written; non-canonical. |
| Class Skills | Each advancing Class has an illustrative list sufficient to validate the current-class-only Skill Investment rule. | Content; non-canonical. |
| Divine casting | Cleric access supplies Divine; each granted Divine Domain supplies an additional specified Tradition. Triel has 17 Spell Slot Advancements. | Divine-Domain access mapping is canonical by DEC-098; slot table and Class content remain open. |
| Tradition Rank | This earlier exercise does not establish a protected primary-Tradition rule. Its former Divine-Legend assumption is superseded by Revision 2, which purchases Tradition Ranks through modeled Magic Mastery-only Investments. | Protected Tradition progression remains possible only as an explicit future Class-table grant. |
| Resources / defenses | Provisional Good, Mediocre, Bad, and None assignments model the source character’s enduring strengths, rather than converting HP and saving-throw modifiers numerically. | Core tracks are canonical; assignments are content. |
| Equipment | The source’s numerical AC, enhancement bonuses, ability-score items, and damage bonuses are replaced by current bounded equipment vectors. | Equipment content remains open. |
| Legacy exceptions | Persistent spells, Divine Metamagic, turn-undead economy, companions, and specific maneuvers become explicit future Features or effects; they do not import 3.5e arithmetic. | Open content / procedure work. |

## 3. Proposed Level-20 Actor Frame

```text
Name: Triel Fitheaben
Ancestry: Human
Alignment / ethos: Neutral Good; martial divine service to Enthariel
Background / origin: Bright Principalities; temple-trained knight and military chaplain
Character Level: 20 (Legend Tier)
Class line: Cleric 4 / Ordained Champion 3 / Knight of the Raven 6 /
            Crusader 1 / Righteous Cohort of Kiri-Jolith 6
Role: armored divine defender, undead hunter, healer, battlefield leader
```

### 3.1 Attribute conversion

The source’s permanent priorities are Wisdom first, then Constitution and Charisma, with solid martial Strength. The projected 3.5e enhancement items do **not** translate as permanent Attribute Dice.

| Attribute | Level 1 | Level 20 proposal | Conversion reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| STR | d6 | d8 | Trained sword-and-shield combatant, not a pure physical specialist. |
| DEX | d4 | d6 | Modest mobility and Reflex support. |
| CON | d6 | d12 | Exceptional hardiness and front-line endurance. |
| INT | d4 | d6 | Formal religious and magical learning. |
| WIS | d8 Primary | d12 Primary | Divine insight, spellcasting, healing, and perception. |
| CHA | d6 | d10 | Command presence, devotional force, and leadership. |

This uses the complete 15-point Secondary Attribute schedule legally:

```text
STR d6 → d8:   2 points
DEX d4 → d6:   1 point
CON d6 → d12:  7 points
INT d4 → d6:   1 point
CHA d6 → d10:  4 points
                —
Total:         15 points
```

Only CON equals the d12 Primary at Level 20, satisfying the one-Secondary-at-Primary limit.

## 5. Divine Casting and Spell Identity

### 5.1 Shared-slot translation

```text
Spell Slot Advancements: 17
Principal protected casting Skill: Divine (target Rank: Legend)
Principal casting Attribute: Wisdom

Domain-granted Tradition access:
  War Domain     → War Tradition; Heavy Blades access for Enthariel's favored weapon
  Healing Domain → Life Tradition
  Law Domain     → Law Tradition
  Sun Domain     → Light Tradition
```

Triel therefore prepares from the fixed spell lists of the individual Traditions he has access to; he does not receive a single Cleric-owned spell list. Domain-to-Tradition mapping is canonical; Domain-Tradition Ranks, number of prepared spells, and the final Spell Slot table remain open content work.

### 5.2 Representative spell conversion portfolio

The source spell list can be preserved as functional packages rather than as 3.5e spell-level arithmetic:

| Source examples | Future system role |
|---|---|
| Cure spells, Heal, Restoration, Delay Death, Vigor | Healing / recovery effects that explicitly state whether they restore Vitality, heal Wounds, remove Conditions, or stabilize. |
| Magic Weapon, Greater Magic Weapon, Divine Power, Holy Transformation | Martial blessings granting explicit Traits, Boons, Damage Boxes, or Permissions; no linear enhancement bonus. |
| Protection from Evil, Freedom of Movement, Death Ward, resist-energy effects | Defensive spells with specified Tags, condition protection, and resource / duration entries. |
| Spiritual Weapon, Flame Strike, Blade Barrier, Sunbeam, Holy Word | Offensive divine effects with their own Activity, defense target, Damage Boxes, Tags, and special consequences. |
| Ice Slick, Shivering Touch, Lava Missile | Explicitly permitted cross-Tradition, Domain, or special-access spells if retained; they should not be silently assumed to be universal Divine spells. |
| Righteous Wrath of the Faithful, Recitation, Valiant Steed, Hero’s Feast | Leadership, morale, travel, and expedition-support magic. |

## 6. Equipment Translation

The source sheet’s `AC 31`, enhancement bonuses, natural armor, deflection bonus, and ability-score items do not transfer directly. The current framework instead gives each item a bounded functional job.

| Source item / benefit | Provisional system expression | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| +3 keen longsword with return crystal | Consecrated longsword with future `Keen` and returning / recall Traits; fixed Damage Boxes still require weapon-catalogue work. | Traits / values open. |
| Heavy armor | Heavy Armor: **4 Damage Absorption**. Additional magical effects should state their own Traits or Permissions. | Absorption 4 is canonical; content open. |
| +5 shield / Parrying Shield | Shield providing the canonical Deflect Boon; its category, Durability, and any special countering Trait are future content. | Baseline shield boon canonical; details open. |

These are conversion targets that preserve the character’s identity. They are not finalized procedures.

| Source feature group | Proposed system-facing expression |
|---|---|

War also grants Heavy Blades access for Enthariel’s favored weapon. They are not universal spell-record mechanics. |
| Destroy / Turn Undead | A `Sun Rite` divine Feature that targets undead through an explicitly defined Willpower or other defense procedure. Its Action, Essence cost, frequency, and destruction threshold remain content work. |

| Raven harrier | A celestial raven companion with a limited harry / distract Activity, future companion rules, and eventually remote spell-delivery Permission. |

## 8. What This Exercise Validates

1. **The 25-Investment schedule works.** Triel has a strong Legend combat specialty, a Hero shield and religious-knowledge profile, and significant breadth without needing any class-granted extra Investments.
2. **Current-Class Skill access is meaningful.** The exact Class taken at Levels 13–20 visibly shapes the skills Triel can advance.
3. **The resource chassis produces an appropriate profile.** `Vitality 25 / Stamina 19 / Essence 26` distinguishes an armored divine champion from a pure martial combatant without importing HP.
4. **Bounded equipment can retain the source identity.** Armor absorbs damage, the shield helps Deflect, and the sword has named Traits—none needs a numerical AC or enhancement-bonus layer.
5. **The remaining gaps are content / subsystem gaps, not a reason to alter the basic character framework.** The major missing pieces are class tables, the Slot table and preparation rules, weapon / shield catalogues, companion rules, and exact spell / feature entries.

## 9. Conversion Questions Raised

This character is a useful future test case for the following open work:

- How protected Skill and defense progression is presented when multiple divine Prestige Classes advance one character concept.
- How Class Skill lists remain readable while permitting a tightly themed multiclass chain.
- The reference Spell Slot table and preparation limits for 17 Slot Advancements.
- The exact treatment of divine turning, smiting, weapon-channeling, persistent magic, and martial maneuvers.
- Domain-granted Tradition Rank and the individual Domain Feature / spell-acquisition packages; Domain-to-Tradition mapping itself is canonical.
- Class-specific Magic Mastery-only Skill Investment grants in representative Class tables.
- Shield categories, magical-equipment effects, and the weapon Damage Box catalogue.
- Celestial companions and command / support Actions.

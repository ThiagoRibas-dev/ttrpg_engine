# Triel Fitheaben — Single Level-by-Level Advancement Matrix

**Status:** Superseded audit artifact.  
**Superseded by:** `05_triel_fitheaben_level_by_level_advancement_matrix_revision_2.md` after DEC-100.  
**Build source:** `03_triel_fitheaben_class_skill_rebuild.md`.  
**Scope:** One matrix combining the currently modeled Character-Level, Class-Level, Attribute, universal Skill, Magic Mastery, Spell Slot, and resource advancement. It does not invent still-unmodeled Class Features, exact defense tables, spell preparation, or weapon values.

> **Correction note:** The original matrix incorrectly advanced Divine and War from Trained to Veteran at Character Level 4. The current Rank cap is Trained at Levels 1–4, so that is illegal. With only six accessible Traditions by Level 3, two Magic Mastery-only Investments at each of Cleric Levels 1–4 create two unspendable Level 4 Investments under the no-banking rule. The matrix is retained as an audit artifact and must not be used as an active build until the Class-table cadence or another explicit exception is decided.

## Assumptions Used

```text
Universal Skill Investments:
  Level 1: 6.
  Levels 2–20: 1 each.

Caster-Class benchmark used for this exercise:
  Every modeled Class Level that grants Spell Slot Advancement
  grants 2 additional Investments restricted to Magic Mastery.

Triel's Classes:
  Cleric 4 / Ordained Champion 3 / Knight of the Raven 6 /
  Crusader 1 / Righteous Cohort of Kiri-Jolith 6.

Primary Attribute:
  WIS d8 at Levels 1–8; d10 at 9–16; d12 at 17–20.
```

`V / S / E` means Vitality, Stamina, and Essence. Resource grants and totals use the provisional Class-track assignments from the earlier Triel conversion exercise.

- Weapon Damage Boxes, shield category, magic-item effects, and Armor Traits.

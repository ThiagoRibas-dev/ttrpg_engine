High Fantasy Advancement & The 6 Tiers of Power

To reflect our game's High Fantasy baseline—where a Level 1 character is already significantly more proficient, resilient, and capable than an average untrained civilian—our system structures character and monster advancement across 20 Core Levels and extends into Epic Progression (Levels 21+), organized across Six Distinct Tiers of Power.

Every advancement level grants concrete, tactile improvements (Step Upgrades, Pool Boons, Feats, and Maneuvers) that apply universally across all actors (Player Characters, NPCs, and Monsters).

1. The High Fantasy Baseline (Level 0 vs Level 1)

In gritty old-school systems, a Level 1 adventurer is a fragile peasant with 4 Hit Points who can be killed by a house cat. In our system, Level 1 actors are already Trained Heroes above the common rabble (DEC-014):

By locking in Model A (DEC-038: Pure Role Separation — Attribute sets Die Size X, Competency Rank sets Pool Volume N and Floor F) while automating primary attribute growth through the Background Focus, we completely eliminate the possibility of a "trap build" while maintaining deep secondary customization.

🏛️ The Dual-Track Attribute Progression Engine (DEC-039 & DEC-040)
text
[ Dual-Track Attribute Progression Engine Over 20 Levels ]
  ├── Track 1: Automatic Background Focus (`Primary Role Ceiling Guarantee`)
  │     └── Your Background (`Blacksmith, Scholar, Soldier`) designates ONE Primary Attribute starting at `d8`.
  │          Automatically steps up at Levels 5, 9, 15, and 20 (`d8 -> d10 -> d12 -> over-cap d12+1 -> Apex d12+2`).
  │
  └── Track 2: Secondary Attribute Step Points (`PF2e-Remaster Cost Allocations`)
        └── At Levels 4, 8, 12, 16, and 20, you receive exactly 2 Step Points to advance secondary stats (`STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA`).
             • Step Cost `1 pt`: Stepping from `d4 -> d6` or `d6 -> d8`.
             • Step Cost `2 pts`: Stepping from `d8 -> d10` or `d10 -> d12`.
Why This Eliminates Trap Builds & Balances Defenses
Bulletproof Primary Ceiling (DEC-039): Because your Background automatically steps your core role attribute up at every Tier boundary (Lvl 5=d10, Lvl 9=d12, Lvl 15=d12+1), a player literally cannot build a weak primary character who falls behind the campaign's Target Numbers!
Generalist vs. Specialist Secondary Costs (DEC-040): Because stepping from d6 -> d8 costs only 1 point, when a character receives 2 Step Points at Level 4, they can easily step two weak attributes up to d8 (say, CON d6 -> d8 and WIS d6 -> d8). This allows characters to naturally bolster their Vitality (HP), Stamina, and Paired Defense Pools (Fortitude STR+CON | Reflexes DEX+INT | Willpower WIS+CHA) without breaking the game! Conversely, pushing a secondary stat to d10 or d12 requires dedicated saving (costing 2 full points).
📈 Summary of How the Check Engine Now Operates (Model A Locked)

When any actor (PC or Monster) makes a check across the 20 levels:

Die Size (X): Set strictly by Core Attribute (d4 to d12+).
Pool Volume (N): Set by Competency Rank (Untrained 2d -> Trained 3d -> Master 4d -> Legend 5d) plus Class/Tactical Boons (+1B/+2B).
Competency Floor (F): Set by Competency Rank (Untrained Floor 0 -> Trained Floor 3 -> Expert Floor 5 -> Master Floor 7 -> Legend Floor 9). Guarantees zero whiffs below your rank.
On-Demand Exertion: Spending 1 Stamina performs a Die Step-Up (d8 -> d10) to clear high difficulty thresholds or reach higher multi-beat counts (DEC-016).

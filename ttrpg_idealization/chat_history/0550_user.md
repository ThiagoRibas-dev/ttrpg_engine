# Turn 550 — User

For Wounds and recovery:

- Treatment: Knowledge — Anatomy and Healing check DC 5. The Spell, Potion, etc. will spell out what it does.
- Post-stabilization: Remains at 0, passed out.
- Wound recovery: Conditions last as long as their Wounds. A Long Rest heals 1 Wound. A Knowledge — Anatomy and Healing check can speed recovery up, at +5 DC to heal one extra Wound. Certain items and spells can speed up Wound healing.
- Medical and magical healing: the Spell, Potion, or other specific effect states what it does.
- Wound escalation: none.
- Bleeding: spend Essence each turn or take Damage.
- Exhaustion/fatigue: only affects expenditure.
- Dazed, Fatigued, and Nauseated: follow the same Wound duration and recovery rules as every other Wound.
- Environmental conditions: deferred.

For spellcasting:

- Each 0th-Circle spell, cantrip, or orison describes its own Action and Essence cost, generally 1 or 2 Essence, and may even use Stamina.
- Metamagic costs depend on the Metamagic.
- Remove spell augmentation as a general procedure; individual spells describe such effects.
- Essence recovers fully after a ten-minute rest such as meditation or prayer, like Stamina.
- Exceptional Spell Slot restoration does not exist by default.

Equipment Durability:

- Items have Durability points depending on the Item.
- Each Magical Effect on an Item increases its Durability by 1.
- At 0 Durability, an Item cannot perform its function.
- Repair uses Craft, magic, or another specific effect.
- Defer or omit general rules for Durability expenditure, replacement, Sunder, armor sacrifice, and magical item strain.

Provisions and campaign resources are deferred.

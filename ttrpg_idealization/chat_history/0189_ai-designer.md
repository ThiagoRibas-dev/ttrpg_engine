Phase 1 Checklist — Foundational Framework and Scaffolding

Project phase: Phase 1 — Foundational Framework and Scaffolding
Authoritative scope record: PROJECT_STATUS.md
Outstanding definitions: 06_brainstorming_logs_and_roadmap/03_outstanding_definitions_index.md

This checklist tracks Phase 1 framework work. It is a project-management artifact, not a source of canonical mechanics. The canonical rules remain in 03_core_baseline_system/ and 04_simulationist_subsystems/.

Status markers:

[x] Completed or sufficiently established for the current framework phase.
[~] In progress, provisional, or partially defined.
[ ] Not yet completed.
1. Project Governance and Documentation
 Establish the canonical/research/conversion/archive directory structure.
 Separate canonical rules from discussion artifacts and deprecated designs.
 Create AGENTS.md with source-of-truth hierarchy and decision-recording protocol.
 Create PROJECT_STATUS.md with current phase, scope boundaries, and exit criteria.
 Create the Outstanding Definitions Index.
 Establish Locked, Provisional, and Open decision-status vocabulary.
 Establish the rule that Agent proposals require explicit User approval before becoming decisions.
 Establish the single-source-of-truth rule for canonical mechanics and terminology.
 Archive the former 65-skill Pulverized Master Matrix.
[~] Perform a complete canonical-document consistency audit.
2. Core Resolution Mathematics
 Establish step-pool resolution as the universal core engine.
 Establish the baseline 2dX keep highest check.
 Establish fixed DC resolution.
 Establish opposed-roll resolution.
 Establish Defender Wins Ties for opposed checks.
 Establish the ordinary die ladder: d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12.
 Remove deprecated d12+, d12+1, and d12+2 terminology from active documentation.
 Establish that the ordinary mathematical ceiling is d12.
 Establish Boons as same-size additional dice.
 Establish Banes as dice removed from the pool.
 Establish that a Bane applied to a one-die pool causes one Die Step-Down.
 Establish that further Banes do not apply additional Die Step-Downs.
 Establish automatic failure when a Bane would step a d4 down.
 Establish that Competency Floors remain active after Bane reduction.
 Establish Die Step-Up and Die Step-Down terminology.
 Remove deprecated Hyper-Shift terminology from active documentation.
 Establish the normal pool ceiling of 5dX.
 Establish Competency Floors.
 Establish the five Competency Ranks.
 Establish complex difficulty notation: DC X (Y).
 Establish that DC X (Y) means Target Number X and Y required successes.
 Establish multi-success resolution as a core framework.
[~] Finalize all Boon/Bane stacking and cancellation edge cases.
[~] Finalize all opposed-roll secondary tie-break procedures.
[~] Finalize the interpretation of exceptional high-tier effects that previously used over-cap dice.
 Complete consolidated probability tables for pool sizes, DCs, Floors, Boons, Banes, and Step-Ups.
 Establish final probability targets for routine, challenging, formidable, heroic, and opposed checks.
 Validate the probability model through dedicated simulations and playtest data.
3. Attributes and Actor Statistics
 Establish the six Attributes: STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA.
 Establish Attributes as die sizes rather than numerical modifiers.
 Establish paired defenses:
 Fortitude: STR + CON.
 Reflexes: DEX + INT.
 Willpower: WIS + CHA.
 Establish the Universal Actor Schema.
 Establish Race/Ancestry, Background/Origin, Class/Prestige Classes, Skills, and Feats as actor blocks.
 Establish Background Primary Attribute Focus.
 Establish Secondary Attribute Step Points as a progression concept.
[~] Finalize all attribute advancement and over-cap replacement effects while remaining bounded by d12.
[~] Finalize resource derivations and advancement.
4. Competency, Skills, and Domains
 Establish Competency Ranks as independent from Character Level.
 Establish individual Skills rather than universal broad checks.
 Establish Domains as game entities that may interact with classes, feats, equipment, and prerequisites.
 Establish the eight current Domains:
 Combat Mastery.
 Athletics, Movement, and Survival.
 Subterfuge and Perception.
 Knowledge and Technical Practice.
 Lore and Cultural Familiarity.
 Social and Influence.
 Magic Mastery.
 Craft and Production.
 Establish Activities and Procedures as distinct from Skills.
 Move Investigation, Forgery, Disguise, Escape Artistry, Lockpicking, Trap Disarming, Shadowing, and similar tasks into the activity/procedure framework.
 Establish open-ended Lore specialties as a concept.
 Establish formal Knowledge skills as a concept.
 Establish Craft specialties as a concept.
 Establish vehicle proficiencies as a concept.
[~] Finalize Craft specialty rank and sheet behavior.
[~] Finalize Lore specialty scope and rank behavior.
[~] Finalize activity procedures, assistance, tools, and multi-skill resolution.
[~] Finalize domain-level mechanics.
5. Character Advancement Architecture
 Establish six High Fantasy Tiers.
 Establish the 20-level mortal progression.
 Establish Mythic as Level 21+.
 Establish Classes and Prestige Classes as the current terminology.
 Establish level-by-level class tables as the authoritative advancement format.
 Establish that Prestige Classes use the same advancement procedure as Base Classes, with entry requirements.
 Establish Class and Prestige Class Spell Slot Advancement entries.
 Establish multiclassing by adding completed Spell Slot Advancements.
[~] Finalize universal class-table column standards.
[~] Finalize the relationship between class features, Feats, Skills, Domains, Attributes, Equipment, and resources.
 Draft representative framework-only class tables for testing; do not yet expand into finalized class content.
6. Action Economy and Combat Scaffolding
 Establish the three-action turn structure.
 Establish the Free Guard reaction.
 Establish five spatial distance tiers.
 Establish Stride as a one-tier movement action.
 Establish active defenses and reaction pressure.
 Establish universal combat maneuvers.
 Establish multi-success maneuver triggers.
 Establish Natural Criticals and Called Shots as distinct mechanics.
 Establish anatomical location and wound concepts.
 Establish Rabble, Underling, and Iconic Champion encounter categories.
[~] Finalize action-cost and multi-action attack fatigue procedures.
[~] Finalize active defense Stamina expenditure.
[~] Finalize combat pacing and encounter calibration.
7. Defenses, Damage, and Wounds
 Establish layered defenses rather than one universal Armor Class.
 Establish Damage Absorption as a post-hit defensive layer.
 Establish Vitality and wound conditions as separate consequences.
 Establish the distinction between Natural Critical wounds and Called Shot wounds.
 Establish severe anatomical conditions as a framework.
[~] Finalize damage-versus-Damage Absorption resolution.
[~] Finalize weapon Damage Dice.
[~] Finalize armor and shield interaction.
[~] Finalize wound severity, recovery, and treatment.
 Run complete combat probability and time-to-defeat calibration after the above rules are stable.
8. Equipment and Economic Scaffolding
[~] Establish the requirement that equipment must use bounded, discrete vectors rather than linear bonuses.
[~] Identify potential equipment vectors: Damage Dice, Damage Absorption, Durability, Traits, critical profiles, and action permissions.
 Define weapon-category and Damage Die framework.
 Define armor-category and Damage Absorption framework.
 Define shields and Parry interaction.
 Define Equipment Durability Slots.
 Define masterwork, magical, and special-material scaling.
 Define crafting, repair, and replacement assumptions.
 Define wealth, equipment access, and campaign-economic expectations by level.
 Decide whether formal wealth-by-level guidance is required.
 Test equipment progression against character, monster, and spell progression.
9. Magic and Spellcasting Scaffolding
 Establish Schools, Traditions, and Traits as the universal spell classifications.
 Remove Mantles as a universal spell axis.
 Establish fixed spell lists by Tradition.
 Establish Classes and Prestige Classes as granting Tradition access rather than class-owned spell lists.
 Establish one universal preparation system.
 Establish one shared daily Spell Slot pool.
 Establish Spell Slot Advancement.
 Establish Spell Slot Progression Level.
 Establish the Reference Good Slot Progression.
 Establish full, every-other-level, and every-four-level advancement cadences as design guidelines.
 Establish Tradition Competency as controlling spell access and intrinsic scaling, not slot access.
 Rename Focus to Essence.
 Keep Essence separate from Stamina.
[~] Define preparation and learned-spell limits.
[~] Define spell-acquisition stacking and multiclass acquisition.
[~] Define Essence expenditure and casting stability.
[~] Define explicit Heightened spell rules.
[~] Define 0th-Circle and Essence interaction.
[~] Define Mythic spell requirements.
 Begin broad spell conversion only after the framework is stable.
10. Conversion and Compatibility Framework
 Establish D&D 3.5e as the foremost compatibility target.
 Establish separate documentation categories for compatibility targets, conversion targets, inspirations, and research references.
 Establish conversion procedures as distinct from canonical rules.
[~] Audit conversion documents for stale mechanics and terminology.
 Validate the framework by converting representative D&D 3.5e material.
 Validate the framework against Pathfinder, SotDL/WW, Mythras/BRP, and tactical content after the core is stable.
11. Phase 1 Exit Review
 Core mathematical framework is internally consistent.
 All ordinary resolution stays within the d12/DC 12 bounded framework.
 Probability targets have been tested and accepted.
 Character progression vectors have distinct mechanical roles.
 Equipment and economic scaffolding is defined.
 Spell-slot and Essence scaffolding is defined.
 Combat, defense, and wound math is calibrated.
 Major outstanding framework questions are resolved or explicitly deferred.
 Canonical documents pass a consistency audit.
 The project is ready to begin systematic content production.
Current Immediate Priorities
Complete Phase 1 mathematical probability calibration.
Define equipment and economic scaffolding.
Finalize activity, Craft, Lore, and Domain procedures.
Complete spell preparation, acquisition, and Essence definitions.
Calibrate combat, damage, and wound math.
Only then begin framework-test classes, feats, spells, ancestries, and monsters.

Mythic expansion remains intentionally deferred until the Level 1–20 framework is stable.

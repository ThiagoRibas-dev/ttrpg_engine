Master Executive Summary: Top-to-Bottom System Architecture

This document provides a concise, comprehensive, top-to-bottom briefing of our Idealized Mathless High Fantasy TTRPG System (as of Decision DEC-029).

It consolidates all mechanics, progression tracks, check engines, combat layers, and resource pools into a single unified view. This overview serves as our design baseline to review, trim, or refactor specific subsystems (such as streamlining Special Effect Tokens [SET] or adjusting how HP/Vitality works).

1. Core Philosophy & Universal Actor Schema
High Fantasy Baseline (DEC-014): Level 1 characters start as Trained Heroes (above average civilian baselines). They are competent professionals capable of reliable execution and heroic survival.
Strictly Zero Table-Side Arithmetic (DEC-001): Action resolution compares rolled die face values (d4 to d12+) directly against Target Numbers (DCs 2 to 12) or opposed die faces (max vs max). There is zero addition or subtraction of linear numerical modifiers (no +BAB, no +Stat mod, no -4 Power Attack).
Strictly Zero Meta-Currencies (DEC-006): All heroic luck, effort, and survival run strictly on in-world character tracks (Stamina, Focus, and Equipment Durability Sacrifices).
Universal Actor Schema (DEC-011): Every single entity in the game world (PCs, human NPCs, Goblins, Ogres, Dragons) is built from the exact same four foundational blocks:

2. The Check Engine (Tri-Vector Pools & Competency Floors)

When any actor declares a check or attack, their dice pool (NdX keep highest) is generated by three variables:

text
[ Assembling a Check Pool: The 3 Mechanical Variables ]
  ├── 1. Die Size (`X` — d4 to d12+) ──────────────► Governs Capability Ceiling & Impact (`Core Attribute + Competency Step Shift`)
  ├── 2. Pool Volume (`N` — 1d to 5d) ─────────────► Governs Reliability & Multi-Beat Count (`Base 2d + Class Boon + Tactical Boons`)
  └── 3. Competency Floor (`F` — 0, 3, 5, 7, 9) ──► Governs Guaranteed Minimum Face (`Investment-Driven Whiff Immunity`)
2.1 Competency Ranks (Investment-Driven Mastery — DEC-020)

Competency Ranks measure specific player investment in one of the 5 Action Mastery Domains (Combat, Survival, Subterfuge, Lore, Social), independent of overall character level:

0. Untrained (Civilian / Zero Investment): Base Pool 2dX keep highest, Floor = 0 (Can whiff on routine tasks).
1. Trained (Basic Professional Focus): Pool Volume 3dX (+1B), Floor = 3 (100% immune to failing DC 3).
2. Expert (Dedicated Specialization): Pool Volume 3dX (+1B) + 1 Die Step-Up (X+1), Floor = 5 (100% immune to failing DC 5).
3. Master (Elite Grandmastery): Pool Volume 4dX (+2B) + 2 Die Step-Ups (X+2), Floor = 7 (100% immune to failing DC 7).
4. Legendary (Mythic Apex): Pool Volume 5dX (+3B — Max Pool Cap) + 3 Die Step-Ups (X+3), Floor = 9 (100% immune to failing DC 9).
2.2 Boons, Banes & Die Stepping (DEC-016 & DEC-017)
Boons (+1B, +2B) & Banes (+1X, +2X): Adjust Pool Volume only (N) by adding or removing dice of the SAME SIZE (2d8 + 1B -> 3d8 keep highest). Banes remove dice (2d8 + 1X -> 1d8 single roll; +2X -> 2d8 keep lowest / Disadvantage). They cancel 1-to-1 before rolling. Max pool ceiling is 5dX.
Die Step-Up (X+1) & Step-Down (X-1): Adjust Die Size only (X). Spending 1 Stamina (or Focus) performs an on-demand Die Step-Up (e.g., 2d6 -> 2d8) to overcome hard DC ceilings or push physical limits. Over-encumbrance or exhaustion imposes a Die Step-Down (d10 -> d8).

4. Attributes & Paired Defenses (DEC-021 & DEC-022)

Characters possess the Classic Six Ability Scores (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA) as step dice (d4 to d12+). Two paired attributes combine directly to form the baseline check pool (1dAttr1 + 1dAttr2 keep highest) for our three saving throw defense layers:

  └── Wisdom (WIS) + Charisma (CHA)          ──► Willpower Pool (`Purely Mental/Spiritual Resilience vs Mind Blasts/Curses`)
Good vs. Poor Class Saves (DEC-029 Track 3): If a class has a Good Save in 3.5e (e.g., Fighter Fortitude, Wizard Willpower), the class awards +1B (+1 die volume -> 3dX) at Level 1, and elevates to +2B (4dX, Floor 5/7) at Level 9 (Master Tier Entry). Poor saves roll standard 2dX paired pools (Floor 0/3).

7. Combat Resolution, Mitigation & Anatomical Wounds (DEC-005, DEC-007, DEC-009)

When an attack connects, it interacts with our 4 defensive layers (Reflexes DEX+INT, Parry Combat Pool, Resilience Fort/Will, and Soak Rank Armor Die). Soak (d4 Cloth to d12 Plate) is checked post-hit (Damage Die Face vs Soak Die Face):

text
[ Attacker Hits Defender ] ──► Compare: `Damage Die Face vs Soak Die Face`
  ├── Damage < Soak ──────────► Absorbed (`0 Wounds`). Defender loses 1 Stamina from jarring shock.
  ├── Damage == Soak ─────────► Superficial Shock. Defender loses 2 Stamina (`0 Wounds`).
  ├── Damage > Soak ──────────► Standard Hit. Defender loses Vitality equal to Damage face + 1 Minor Wound.
  └── Critical Hit (`Max Face`) ► Severe Lethal Hit. Double Vitality loss (`Max x 2`) + Severe Anatomical Wound (`1d8 Matrix`).
7.1 Hit Location & Anatomical Wound Severance (The 1d8 Location Matrix)

When an attack achieves a Standard Hit (Damage > Soak) or Critical Hit (Damage rolled Max Face), roll 1d8 to pinpoint where the blow lands and what structural impairment occurs:

1 (Head): Concussion / Dazed (INT/WIL step down d10 -> d8; drop active spell concentration).
2-3 (Chest): Winded (Lose 3 Stamina instantly; spending Stamina hurts).
4 (Abdomen): Gut Trauma (+1 Bane on physical checks + Bleeding Cut: -1 HP/round).
5 (Shield Arm): Limp Shield Arm (Drop shield/secondary item immediately in adjacent zone).
6 (Weapon Arm): Limp Weapon Arm (Drop primary weapon instantly; off-hand takes +2 Banes).
7-8 (Legs): Buckled Knee (Fall Prone immediately; Speed 0 until standing up via [1A]).

Token Generation: For every additional die in the winner's pool that meets or exceeds the loser's highest roll (Multi-Beat Count), award +1 [SET]. If the winner rolled Max Die Face, award +1 [SET].

8. Class Advancement Tracks & 20-Level Progression (DEC-013 & DEC-029)

Character and monster growth from Level 1 to 20 (and Epic 21+) is organized across 6 High Fantasy Tiers (Trained Lvl 1-4 -> Veteran Lvl 5-8 -> Master Lvl 9-12 -> Hero Lvl 13-16 -> Legend Lvl 17-20 -> Mythic Lvl 21+).

All 5 classic D&D 3.5e class tracks translate cleanly across these tiers:

Hit Points (HP): Bounded increments (+2 HP Martial, +1 Caster) at odd levels.
Base Attack Bonus (BAB): Tri-Vector pool step/volume growth (Full Martial reaches Expert Floor 5 at Lvl 5, Master Floor 7 at Lvl 9, Legend Floor 9 at Lvl 15).
Saving Throws (Fort, Ref, Will): Good saves award +1B at Level 1 and +2B at Level 9. Poor saves roll base 2dX paired pools.
Caster Level (CL): Focus increment (+2 to +3 Focus/level). Tradition Competency sets check pool (Trained 3dX/Floor 3 to Legend 5dX+3 Steps/Floor 9). Spells scale via Die Step-Ups across Distance Tiers.
Skill Points: Domain Proficiency Allocations awarded across levels (Levels 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 10, 12, 14, 17, 18) to advance Action Mastery Ranks (Untrained to Legendary).

Now that the entire system is laid out cleanly in front of us, let's look at the exact areas where you mentioned we might want to simplify or refactor:

The Special Effect Token ([SET]) Engine:
Current Complexity: Checking multi-beat differentials (counting how many extra dice beat the opponent's roll) plus max-face checks every turn can sometimes slow down casual tables if players are deciding how to spend 2-3 tokens across a table of 8 options.
Potential Streamlining / Trim: We could simplify [SET] tokens into Direct Degree of Success Triggers (e.g., if you roll Max Face OR beat the opponent with 2+ dice, you simply choose ONE maneuver instantly from a clean 4-item list: Impale, Bleed, Knockdown, or Disarm). That eliminates token counting entirely!
Hit Points / Vitality vs. Wounds:
Current Complexity: We currently track both Vitality (HP) subtraction (e.g., 18 HP - 6 damage = 12 HP) AND our 1d8 Anatomical Wound Matrix (when Damage > Soak).
Potential Streamlining / Trim: We could eliminate HP numerical tracking completely if desired, making combat 100% Condition & Wound Driven (e.g., if Damage > Soak, take 1 Anatomical Wound checkmark; if 3 Wounds accumulated OR Critical hit -> Out of action). Or conversely, keep Vitality (HP) and reserve 1d8 Anatomical Wounds strictly for Critical Hits!
Defense Layer Resolution:
Current Complexity: Checking Evasion/Parry vs Soak post-hit.
Potential Streamlining / Trim: We can review how smoothly active Parry/Reflexes transitions into post-hit Damage vs Soak to make sure it plays lightning fast.

# Turn 15 — User

We seem to be working with 2dx as a baseline. How are we deciding where a character has more or less die of higher or lower size?

For example, let's compare:

a strong melee class who is proficient in his weapon of choice

a weak melee class who is proficient in his weapon of choice

a strong melee class who is not proficient in his weapon of choice

a strong non-melee class who is proficient in his weapon of choice

Of course, that is 1 consideration. The game should not work only based on the chance of success, but also options characters have such as a melee combatant class having access to Power Attack and the like, but this is a important consideration. We could decide that only being strong and proficient affect your pool while your class gives you abilities, or vice versa. Let's brainstorm the possible scenarios.

Strong and weak in here could be a function of race + attributes (which could be the die size or something like that?)

We need to come up with a system that's meaningful, while being somewhat granular so that players can not only invest into but advance over the 20 levels (once every 4 levels for example) by virtue of their choices.

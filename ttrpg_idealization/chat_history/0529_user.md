# Turn 529 — User

Stacking will follow the Type rules as already defined. There's no rule to remove these early, specific Abilities and Effects may specify that they do so.

An Attack against a Shaken target receives the Boon even when it cannot normally perceive or affect that target. It represents the affected Actor fumbling on Evading, Deflecting, etc.

Full Defense permits a creature to spend Stamina on Interpose’s required Deflect in addition to its 2-Stamina base cost.

Exert applies to Dex- and Str-based checks or rolls. A d12 character can Step-Up to a d20; this is a temporary Exert exception to the ordinary d12 Attribute ceiling.

Charge moves up to Speed and targets a single Actor.

Cleave requires a melee weapon and cannot target the same creature more than once.

Execution against a target above 0 Vitality allows Deflect, Evasion, Interpose, and Soften Blow normally. Its purpose is to kill a target outright if the Execution Strike reduces it to 0 Vitality, rather than requiring a later finishing action.

High Fantasy Advancement & The 6 Tiers of Power

To reflect our game's High Fantasy baseline—where a Level 1 character is already significantly more proficient, resilient, and capable than an average untrained civilian—our system structures character and monster advancement across 20 Core Levels and extends into Epic Progression (Levels 21+), organized across Six Distinct Tiers of Power.

Every advancement level grants concrete, tactile improvements (Step Upgrades, Pool Boons, Feats, and Maneuvers) that apply universally across all actors (Player Characters, NPCs, and Monsters).

1. The High Fantasy Baseline (Level 0 vs Level 1)

In gritty old-school systems, a Level 1 adventurer is a fragile peasant with 4 Hit Points who can be killed by a house cat. In our system, Level 1 actors are already Trained Heroes above the common rabble:

Metric	Level 0 Civilian / Average Joe (Untrained)	Level 1 Adventurer / Trained Hero (High Fantasy Baseline)
Core Attributes	All Attributes at d4 or d6	Primary Attributes at d8, Secondary at d6.
Action Mastery	Untrained (0B) across all domains (2dX base pool).	Trained (+1B -> 3dX keep highest) in 2 primary domains.
Defense & Soak	Evasion d4, Parry d4, Soak d4 (Cloth)	Evasion d8, Parry d8 (2d8), Soak d6-d10 (Leather to Heavy Mail).
Resource Pools	6 Vitality, 8 Stamina, 8 Focus	14 Vitality (HP), 16 Stamina, 16 Focus Pool.
Maneuver Access	Zero maneuver training (Basic attacks only).	Full access to Universal Special Effects ([SET]) + 2 Feats (1 Racial + 1 General/Class).

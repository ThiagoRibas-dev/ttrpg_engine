This document maintains our running log of design decisions, open architectural questions, and actionable roadmap items for the iterative evolution of our idealized mathless TTRPG system.

## 2. Open Architectural Questions & Dilemmas

### Dilemma 1: Managing Pool Size Bloat vs Counting Fatigue
- **The Issue:** While `2dX` base pools with up to $+3\text{ Boons (`5dX`)}$ are fast and tactile, what happens when a character has legendary domain mastery (`+3B`), flanking (`+1B`), high ground (`+1B`), and a spell buff (`+1B`) simultaneously? Does rolling `8dX keep highest` slow down table play or obscure dice faces?
- **Proposed Solution:** Establish a strict **Pool Volume Ceiling (`Max 5 Dice in any check pool`)**. Any excess Boons beyond `5dX` automatically convert into a free **Die Step-Up (`d8` $\to$ `d10`)** or grant an immediate **Secondary Action / Stride (`[1A]`)** due to overwhelming tactical dominance.

### Dilemma 2: Magic Item & Equipment Scaling
- **The Issue:** In D&D 3.5e and PF2e, characters scale from normal steel swords up to $+5\text{ Vorpal Greatswords}$ and $+5\text{ Full Plate}$. If we eliminate $+1 / +2$ enhancement bonuses, how do masterwork and magical weapons scale?
- **Proposed Solution:** Magic weapons scale across **Three Concrete Vectors**:
  1. **Durability Slot Expansion:** (`Standard: 2 Slots` $\to$ `Adamantine: 4 Slots`, allowing more heroic sacrifices).
  2. **Soak Penetration:** (`Mithril/Magic blades automatically step down enemy Soak Ranks by 1 rank`).
  3. **Embedded Keyword / Trigger Traits:** (`[Vorpal]`, `[Searing]`, `[Vampiric]`), which trigger concrete status effects on Critical Successes without altering core numbers.
